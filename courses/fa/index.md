# Master Summary: Mastering Fundamental Analysis (CC-FA)

---

### 1. OVERARCHING THEME & OBJECTIVE

The core objective of the **Mastering Fundamental Analysis** module is to equip equity investors with a rigorous, evidence-based methodology to eliminate capital-impairing traps and identify high-conviction multi-bagger compounders in the Indian stock market (FA-Lec01, CC-FA-Lec05). The module transitions learners from static accounting audits to analyzing dynamic fundamental rates of change—specifically tracking balance sheet expansion, operating leverage inflections, cash flow conversion efficiency, and quarterly earnings acceleration (FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec04). By integrating quantitative screening filters (CANSLIM, Relative Strength), technical Stage 2 breakout timing, forensic governance checks, and scenario-based valuation modeling, the course establishes an end-to-end investment execution framework that protects capital during market downturns while capturing upside from P/E re-ratings and earnings expansion (CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).

The sub-course directly develops all four pillars of the **TVGP Framework** (Theme, Valuation, Growth, Promoter) across five of its six lectures, with one lecture dedicated strictly to financial statement integration (FA-Lec01, FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06):
*   **Theme**: Explored through macro supply-demand capital cycles (shortage-glut dynamics), Value-Added Product (VAP) mix transitions, transmission/green energy capex multipliers, and distinguishing structural moats from transitory policy protections (FA-Lec01, CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **Valuation**: Cultivated by moving from static P/E multiples to Growth-At-A-Reasonable-Price (GARP) bands (15x–30x P/E), 3–4 year forward capacity P/E modeling, forward PEG ratios (<1.5x), asset-light vs. asset-heavy EV/EBITDA comparisons, and Bull/Bear/Base scenario exit testing (FA-Lec01, FA-Lec02, CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **Growth**: Systematized by tracking "NOS" fundamental catalysts (new CapEx, new products, new geographies, client orders, regulations), 3-factor DuPont ROE decomposition, operating leverage mechanics, and CANSLIM quarterly sales (>15%) and PAT (>20%) accelerations (FA-Lec01, FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **Promoter**: Audited through forensic checks including Asset-to-Order red flags (<1%), CFO salary anomalies (<₹50,000/qtr), warrant regulatory terms (25% cash upfront, 75% within 18 months), promoter equity offloading alerts (>5% in 30 days), and persistent insider liquidation (FA-Lec01, FA-Lec02, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
*   *Note on FA-Lec03*: Explicitly designated as "Not TVGP-specific" in the course references, FA-Lec03 instead serves as the crucial structural bridge connecting the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement to evaluate capital structure evolution and cash conversion (FA-Lec03).

### 3. CORE PILLARS OF THE MODULE

#### Pillar 1: Forensic Elimination & Capital Protection Filter
*   **Why it matters**: Before evaluating growth or technical entry points, investors must execute forensic checks to discard fraudulent enterprises, operator-cornered low-float stocks, and uncollected revenue traps. Protecting capital from bankruptcy or illiquidity destruction takes precedence over seeking returns (FA-Lec01, FA-Lec03).
*   **How it develops across lectures**: Introduced in **FA-Lec01** (forensic checks: Asset-to-Order ratio <1%, CFO salary <₹50,000/qtr, low-float 400x–900x P/E traps) → Applied in **FA-Lec02** (auditing consolidated vs. standalone debt traps, low-priced debt traps) → Refined in **FA-Lec03** (uncollected receivable spikes leading to write-offs and bankruptcy) → Screened in **CC-FA-Lec04** (percentage delivery fallacies, micro-cap computer screening traps) → Integrated in **CC-FA-Lec05** (promoter stake offloading >5% alert, peak valuation QIPs) → Operationalized in **CC-FA-Lec06** (persistent promoter liquidation as an immediate exclusion filter).
*   **Key rules/thresholds**: Asset-to-Order <1% red flag (FA-Lec01); CFO Salary <₹50,000/qtr for ₹30,000 cr market cap (FA-Lec01); Debtor Days expanding 10–13 years (FA-Lec03); Micro-cap <₹100 Cr without AGM attendance (CC-FA-Lec04); Promoter Selling >5% in 30 days (CC-FA-Lec05).
*   **Read more**: FA-Lec01 (Report Stage 2), FA-Lec02 (Report Stage 8, 10), FA-Lec03 (Report Stage 11), CC-FA-Lec04 (Report Stage 9), CC-FA-Lec05 (Report Stage 9), CC-FA-Lec06 (Report Stage 11, 12).

#### Pillar 2: Three-Statement Cash Flow Conversion & Self-Funding Capital Structure
*   **Why it matters**: Accrual accounting net profit (PAT) can mask severe cash starvation caused by working capital lockups in inventory and receivables, forcing equity dilution or terminal debt traps. Evaluating true financial health requires verifying that operating earnings convert into real Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) to self-fund expansion (FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03).
*   **How it develops across lectures**: Introduced in **FA-Lec01** (CFO omission in Rajesh Exports vs. cash generation in Sting expansion) → Applied in **FA-Lec02** (CFO/EBITDA >60% and CFO/PAT >90% benchmarks; GNFC deleveraging debt from ₹2,000 cr to ₹34 cr) → Refined in **FA-Lec03** (3-statement reconciliation, establishing B2B CFO/PAT ≥60%–70% and B2C ≥80%–90% cutoffs; contrasting Neogen's QIP dilution with Pricol's self-funded ₹400 cr Capex) → Systematized in **CC-FA-Lec05** (10-year average CFO/PAT >80% checklist threshold; Neogen's negative cumulative CFO of -₹13 cr vs ₹212 cr PAT).
*   **Key rules/thresholds**: CFO/EBITDA ≥60% (FA-Lec02) / ≥80% (FA-Lec03); CFO/PAT ≥60%–70% for B2B and ≥80%–90% for B2C (FA-Lec03); 10-Year Avg CFO/PAT >80% (CC-FA-Lec05); Debt-to-Equity <0.7x healthy, >2.0x high risk (FA-Lec02) / D/E ceiling ≤1.25x (FA-Lec03) / <1.0x (CC-FA-Lec05).
*   **Read more**: FA-Lec01 (Report Stage 2, 10), FA-Lec02 (Report Stage 8, 9, 10), FA-Lec03 (Report Stage 1, 2, 3, 11), CC-FA-Lec05 (Report Stage 8, 9).

#### Pillar 3: Operating Leverage, Capacity Utilization & Asset Efficiency
*   **Why it matters**: Explosive profit expansion occurs when rising plant utilization absorbs fixed overhead costs, or when high asset-turnover models scale revenue without heavy capital commitments. Tracking CWIP commercialization, utilization steps, and unit-level EBITDA margins exposes earnings inflections before headline profits reflect full recovery (FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03).
*   **How it develops across lectures**: Introduced in **FA-Lec01** (VAP OPM +5.5% delta in Time Technoplast; Capital Cycle Shortage-Glut Model) → Applied in **FA-Lec02** (Capacity Utilization P&L Forecasting 20% to 90%; Yasho Industries tripling fixed block; Fixed Asset Turnover <2x asset-heavy vs >2x asset-light) → Refined in **FA-Lec03** (Shilchar 10x asset turn vs Aarti operating deleverage; Newgen soft leverage; DuPont ROCE optimization) → Screened in **CC-FA-Lec04** (Balance Sheet CWIP acceleration vs Net Fixed Assets) → Deconstructed in **CC-FA-Lec05** (3-Factor DuPont ROE NPM × Asset Turn × Leverage and 2-Factor ROCE EBIT Margin × Capital Employed Turnover; Thangamayil 24.9% ROE via asset turn) → Projected in **CC-FA-Lec06** (Forward capacity P/E modeling; Ashiana Housing delivery delays unlocking margin).
*   **Key rules/thresholds**: Fixed Asset Turnover <2x (Asset-Heavy) vs >2x (Asset-Light) (FA-Lec02); Asset Turn Benchmark 1.5x (FA-Lec03); Corporate ROE & ROCE Target >15% (CC-FA-Lec05); Greenfield Hospital Breakeven 5–6 yrs (FA-Lec03); CWIP Acceleration Ratio (CC-FA-Lec04).
*   **Read more**: FA-Lec01 (Report Stage 4, 7), FA-Lec02 (Report Stage 3, 4, 9, 11, 13), FA-Lec03 (Report Stage 4, 5, 6, 8), CC-FA-Lec04 (Report Stage 9), CC-FA-Lec05 (Report Stage 3, 4, 6, 7), CC-FA-Lec06 (Report Stage 5, 12).

#### Pillar 4: Fundamental Rate of Change & Growth Accelerators ("New" Catalysts, CANSLIM, PEED)
*   **Why it matters**: Sustainable stock outperformance requires identifying dynamic operational rate-of-change catalysts ("New" CapEx, new products, regulations, client orders) rather than relying on historical growth. Combining quarterly growth acceleration with post-earnings drift captures multi-quarter P/E re-ratings (CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **How it develops across lectures**: Introduced in **FA-Lec01** ("NOS" catalysts driving earnings acceleration and P/E re-rating; BRM concall framework) → Applied in **FA-Lec02** (7-point Income Statement analysis tracking quarterly sales/PAT acceleration; CANSLIM 8–12 quarter horizon) → Systematized in **CC-FA-Lec04** (CANSLIM 'C' YoY Sales >15% and YoY PAT >20%; "New" catalysts like Sarda Energy IBC acquisition; 5–6 quarter earnings explosion runway) → Integrated in **CC-FA-Lec05** (PG Electroplast 50% forward PAT growth combined with PLI incentives) → Operationalized in **CC-FA-Lec06** (Post Earnings Announcement Drift [PEED] strategy; Zen Technologies 200–300% PAT explosion compressing 70–75x P/E).
*   **Key rules/thresholds**: YoY Sales Growth >15% and YoY PAT Growth >20% (CC-FA-Lec04); CANSLIM Horizon 8–12 qtrs (FA-Lec02); Earnings Explosion Runway 5–6 qtrs (CC-FA-Lec04); Institutional Lapping FII/DII >+0.5% (CC-FA-Lec04) / FII & DII Change >+1–2% (CC-FA-Lec04).
*   **Read more**: FA-Lec01 (Report Stage 8, 10), FA-Lec02 (Report Stage 12, 13), CC-FA-Lec04 (Report Stage 1, 2, 4, 6, 8), CC-FA-Lec05 (Report Stage 12), CC-FA-Lec06 (Report Stage 9).

#### Pillar 5: Technical Integration, Stage Analysis & Risk-Managed Exits
*   **Why it matters**: Fundamental strength must be synchronized with price trend confirmation and institutional float absorption to avoid value traps or early entries. Enforcing technical entry filters (Stage 2 breakouts, triple confirmation) and rigid exit rules (weekly VSTOP) prevents severe capital drawdowns (CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **How it develops across lectures**: Introduced in **FA-Lec01** (avoiding cornered low-float traps; price-volume technical entry background) → Applied in **CC-FA-Lec04** (Stan Weinstein 4-Stage Analysis focusing on Stage 2 breakouts above 30-week MA; 26-week Relative Strength vs Nifty 50; Volume Rocketing 10-day avg >1.5x 20-day avg) → Refined in **CC-FA-Lec05** (Triple Technical Confirmation: 10/30 EMA Golden Crossover, weekly RSI >50, 26-week green RS; Monthly RSI <50 top signal in Asian Paints) → Extended in **CC-FA-Lec06** (Weekly VSTOP 2x multiplier exit framework for >100x P/E or decelerating stocks; 2–4–6–8% position sizing framework).
*   **Key rules/thresholds**: Volume Rocketing 10-day Avg Vol >1.5x 20-day Avg Vol (CC-FA-Lec04); 26-Week Relative Strength 52-week high / green (CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05); Weekly RSI >50 & Monthly RSI <50 top signal (CC-FA-Lec05); Weekly VSTOP Exit = 2 consecutive weekly closes below (CC-FA-Lec06); Position Sizing: 2–4% unproven turnarounds, 6–8% high-conviction market leaders (CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **Read more**: FA-Lec01 (Report Stage 2), CC-FA-Lec04 (Report Stage 6, 7, 9), CC-FA-Lec05 (Report Stage 10), CC-FA-Lec06 (Report Stage 7).

#### Pillar 6: Valuation Frameworks & Forward Multiple Modeling (GARP / PEG / Scenario P/E)
*   **Why it matters**: Overpaying for growth without verifying forward capacity or margin sustainability exposes portfolios to severe multiple compression losses. Investors must anchor to growth-at-a-reasonable-price (GARP) bands, conduct Bear case downside testing, and model multi-year forward capacity P/E and PEG ratios (CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **How it develops across lectures**: Introduced in **FA-Lec01** (Peter Lynch categories: Slow Growers <10–12%, Stalwarts 10–12%, Fast Growers >20–30%, Cyclicals, Turnarounds, Asset Plays; P/E re-rating) → Applied in **CC-FA-Lec04** (GARP valuation band 15x–30x P/E, max 40x; SAMHI vs Indian Hotels EV/EBITDA contrast) → Structured in **CC-FA-Lec05** (Bull/Bear/Base scenario valuation modeling; Forward PEG Ratio <1.5x, target 1.0x–1.2x; Nifty 50 entry P/E <20.0x TTM @ 22,000–22,200) → Operationalized in **CC-FA-Lec06** (3–4 Year Forward P/E & PEG ballpark model; EV = Market Cap + Total Debt - Cash; avoiding >100x–150x P/E EMS high multiple traps).
*   **Key rules/thresholds**: GARP Valuation Band 15x–30x P/E (Max 40x) (CC-FA-Lec04); Forward PEG Ratio <1.5x (Target 1.0x–1.2x) (CC-FA-Lec05); Nifty 50 Entry Level P/E <20.0x TTM (CC-FA-Lec05); EV = Market Cap + Total Debt - Cash (CC-FA-Lec06); Multi-Year Forward P/E Modeling (CC-FA-Lec06).
*   **Read more**: FA-Lec01 (Report Stage 9), CC-FA-Lec04 (Report Stage 5, 8), CC-FA-Lec05 (Report Stage 1, 11, 12, 13), CC-FA-Lec06 (Report Stage 10, 12).

#### b) Curriculum Progression Narrative
**FA-Lec01** establishes the foundational mindset by introducing forensic elimination rules (Asset-to-Order anomalies, salary checks), supply-demand capital cycle dynamics, and the TVGP framework (FA-Lec01). This prepares the ground for **FA-Lec02** and **FA-Lec03**, which provide the core statement tools. **FA-Lec02** details the 7-Point Balance Sheet and 7-Point Income Statement frameworks, capacity utilization forecasting, and ratio cutoffs (Debt-to-Equity <0.7x, Asset Turn ``````<2x vs >``````2x) (FA-Lec02). **FA-Lec03** bridges accrual accounting to cash reality through the Three-Statement Integration Framework, establishing mandatory CFO conversion benchmarks (B2B 60–70%, B2C 80–90%) and operating leverage mechanics that link fixed asset growth to PAT expansion (FA-Lec03).

With tools established, **CC-FA-Lec04** transitions to automated application by constructing quantitative stock screening engines that combine CANSLIM quarterly acceleration (>15% Sales, >20% PAT), "New" fundamental catalysts, and Stan Weinstein Stage 2 technical breakouts with 26-week Relative Strength (CC-FA-Lec04). **CC-FA-Lec05** synthesizes these pillars into an integrated 4-part research checklist framework (Fundamentals, Technicals, Management, Valuation), applying 3-Factor DuPont ROE decomposition, triple technical confirmation (EMA crossover, RSI >50, green RS), and Bull/Bear/Base scenario valuation modeling (PEG <1.5x) (CC-FA-Lec05). Finally, **CC-FA-Lec06** elevates the curriculum to advanced market execution through Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEED) scans, forward capacity P/E modeling, strict weekly VSTOP exit triggers, and 2–4–6–8% portfolio position sizing to navigate real-world market cycles (CC-FA-Lec06).

### 5. MASTER FRAMEWORK / CHECKLIST

1.  **Macro & Index Valuation Assessment**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Nifty 50 TTM P/E & Market Cap to GDP Ratio.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Require Nifty 50 P/E < 20.0x TTM (approx. 22,000–22,200 level) for low-risk market entry; differentiate macro index valuation from hyper-growth compounders (CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
    *   *Source*: CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06.
2.  **Quantitative Screening & Automated Filter**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: CANSLIM 'C' & 'I' Screen & 26-Week Relative Strength Filter.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: YoY Sales Growth > 15%, YoY PAT Growth > 20%, ROCE > 15%, Market Cap > ₹1,000 Cr; FII/DII QoQ accumulation > +0.5% (or +1–2% query); 26-week Relative Strength line at 52-week high / outperforming Nifty 50 (CC-FA-Lec04).
    *   *Source*: CC-FA-Lec04.
3.  **Forensic Elimination Audit**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Forensic Red Flag Screening (Asset-to-Order Ratio, Salary Anomalies, Promoter Selling, Debtor Spikes).
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Asset-to-Order Ratio ≥ 1% (reject if Assets < 1% of orders); CFO salary reasonable for market cap; Promoter selling ≤ 5% in 30 days; Debtor days stable (reject expanding debtor days 10–13 years); reject illiquid low-float cornering traps (400x–900x P/E with negative CFO) (FA-Lec01, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
    *   *Source*: FA-Lec01, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06.
4.  **Balance Sheet & Financial Health Audit**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: 7-Point Balance Sheet Analysis & Debt-to-Equity Cap.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Debt-to-Equity healthy < 0.7x, high-risk cutoff > 2.0x (D/E ceiling ≤ 1.25x; ideal < 1.0x); Net Debt = Borrowings - (Cash + Cash Equivalents); Fixed Asset Turnover (< 2x Asset-Heavy vs > 2x Asset-Light); analyze Consolidated statements if subsidiaries exist (FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05).
    *   *Source*: FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05.
5.  **Three-Statement Cash Flow Conversion & Liquidity Reconciliation**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Three-Statement Integration Framework & CFO Conversion Benchmarks.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: CFO ÷ PAT ≥ 60%–70% for B2B models, ≥ 80%–90% for B2C models (10-year average CFO/PAT > 80%); CFO ÷ EBITDA ≥ 60% (FA-Lec02) / ≥ 80% (FA-Lec03); monitor Cash Conversion Cycle (Receivables + Inventory - Payables) (FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05).
    *   *Source*: FA-Lec02, FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05.
6.  **Fundamental Catalysts & Operating Leverage Analysis**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Fundamental Rate of Change ("New" Catalysts), CWIP Acceleration & Capacity Utilization P&L Model.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Identify operational catalysts ("New" CapEx, product upgrades, IBC acquisitions, VAP mix shift with +5.5% OPM delta); map plant capacity utilization steps (20% to 90%) against fixed overheads; monitor Balance Sheet CWIP acceleration vs Net Fixed Assets for 5–6 quarter earnings runway (FA-Lec01, FA-Lec02, CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec06).
    *   *Source*: FA-Lec01, FA-Lec02, CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec06.
7.  **ROE/ROCE Decomposition & Moat Evaluation**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: 3-Factor DuPont ROE & 2-Factor ROCE Decomposition.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: ROE Target > 15% (decomposed into NPM × Asset Turn × Financial Leverage); ROCE Target > 15% (decomposed into EBIT Margin × Capital Employed Turnover); verify moats are structural rather than transitory policy/anti-dumping protections (FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05).
    *   *Source*: FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05.
8.  **Technical Confirmation & Entry Timing**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Stan Weinstein 4-Stage Analysis & Triple Technical Confirmation.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Confirm stock is in Stage 2 (Advancing above 30-week MA); 10-week/30-week EMA Golden Crossover; weekly RSI > 50; 26-week green RS vs Nifty 50; Volume Rocketing (10-day Avg Vol > 1.5x 20-day Avg Vol or 5-day volume > 50% above 10-day avg) (CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05).
    *   *Source*: CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05.
9.  **Scenario Valuation & Forward Multiple Modeling**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: GARP Valuation Band, 3–4 Year Forward P/E & Forward PEG Model.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Anchor to GARP valuation band (15x–30x P/E, max 40x); project 3–4 year forward PAT from capacity expansion; require Forward PEG Ratio < 1.5x (target 1.0x–1.2x); run Bull, Bear, and Base scenario exit P/E models to ensure Bear case preserves capital (CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
    *   *Source*: CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06.
10. **Position Sizing & Risk Allocation**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: 2–4–6–8% Position Sizing Framework.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Allocate 2%–4% of cost to unproven order book turnarounds/commodity stories; allocate 6%–8% of cost to market leaders with 50%–60% earnings growth visibility over 8–10 quarters (CC-FA-Lec06).
    *   *Source*: CC-FA-Lec06.
11. **Ongoing Risk Monitoring & Exit Execution**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Weekly VSTOP Exit Framework & Peak Margin / RSI Top Alerts.
    *   *Condition/Threshold*: Enforce Weekly VSTOP (2x multiplier) exit on 2 consecutive weekly closes below for extended stocks (>100x P/E) or decelerating growth; exit if Monthly RSI < 50; exit if operating margins hit unsustainable peak vs normalized guidance (e.g., 26% current vs 15% normalized PBT) (CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
    *   *Source*: CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06.

### 8. COMMON MISTAKES & RED FLAGS ACROSS THE MODULE

1.  **Extrapolating Cyclical Peak Margins & Shortage Earnings**: Buying commodity or B2B/defense producers at peak shortage margins (HEG, Laurus, Mazagon Dock 26%–27% current vs 15% normalized PBT) without verifying multi-year OPM baselines on Screener or management's normalized guidance (FA-Lec01, CC-FA-Lec06).
2.  **Blind Institutional Cloning & Retail Over-Allocation**: Aping super-investors or institutional funds into distressed/turnaround stocks (DHFL, Yes Bank) where retail investors allocate 20%–30% of their portfolio compared to the fund's 1% position size (FA-Lec01).
3.  **Ignoring Balance Sheet Debt Traps & Standalone Accounting**: Buying low-priced stocks with ballooning debt, negative reserves, or evaluating standalone statements when subsidiaries exist (which conceal total consolidated debt and enterprise liabilities) (FA-Lec02).
4.  **Uncollected Receivable Spikes & Working Capital Cash Drain**: Ignoring expanding debtor days (e.g., 10–13 years in Opto Circuits/Bartronics), which forces short-term debt, spikes interest costs, leads to massive write-offs, negative cash flow, and bankruptcy (FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05).
5.  **Frequent Equity Dilution & Bloated Cash Hoarding**: Relying on frequent QIP equity raises to fund operations (Neogen Chemicals) or hoarding excess idle cash (>50% of assets yielding ~5% like Avanti Feeds or un-deployed QIP cash in PI Industries), which severely dilutes Return on Equity (ROE) (FA-Lec03, CC-FA-Lec05).
6.  **Catching Falling Knives in Stage 4 Downtrends**: Averaging down in declining stocks trading below a declining 30-week moving average (Asian Paints, TCI Express) undergoing technical distribution, margin contraction, or quick-commerce disruption (DMart) (FA-Lec02, CC-FA-Lec04, CC-FA-Lec06).
7.  **Micro-Cap Computer Screening & Low-Float Traps**: Trading illiquid stocks under ₹100 Cr market cap without attending AGMs, or entering illiquid 400x–900x P/E stocks with negative cash flows (-₹96 cr), exposing portfolios to operator cornering and fake volume (FA-Lec01, CC-FA-Lec04).
8.  **Mistaking Transitory Policy Protections for Moats**: Confusing government anti-dumping duties or temporary trade barriers (Borosil Renewables, Waaree Energies) with structural competitive moats (CC-FA-Lec05).
9.  **Chasing Peak Valuation QIPs & Extended Multiple Traps**: Buying institutional QIPs or EMS stocks at extended multiples above 100x–150x P/E (IndiaMART at 105x, PG Electroplast above 150x), where a single quarter's growth deceleration causes 40%+ drawdowns and long-term multiple compression losses (CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
10. **Ignoring Promoter Stake Liquidation & Exclusivity Cliffs**: Holding stocks where promoters offload >5% stake in 30 days during operational headwinds or speculative rallies (EaseMyTrip, MTAR, Waaree Tech), or holding single-molecule pharma stocks facing First-to-File exclusivity cliff falls (Natco Pharma Revlimid Q1 FY26 cliff) (CC-FA-Lec05, CC-FA-Lec06).
