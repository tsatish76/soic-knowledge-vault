# Sub-Course Master Summary: Filter A Stock (FAS)

## 1. OVERARCHING THEME & OBJECTIVE

The **Filter A Stock (FAS)** module builds a repeatable, end-to-end stock discovery and filtration engine across ~5,000+ listed Indian equities and global markets (US, Europe, Japan, and South Korea) (FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec03, FAS-Lec04). Its core objective is to replace slow manual screening—which takes roughly four months to cover the Indian universe—with automated quantitative and qualitative scanners that surface businesses undergoing a genuine fundamental rate of change anchored by a public **"NEW" catalyst** (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04). By pairing automated discovery (StockScans, Screener.in, MarketSmith, TradingView, IBD 50, and Perplexity Max) with Stan Weinstein's Stage Analysis, Relative Strength, and William O'Neil's price-volume rules, an investor learns to convert raw scan hits into high-conviction watchlists while systematically avoiding Stage 4 falling knives and deep-value traps (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec03, FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05).

Within the **TVGP (Theme, Valuation, Growth, Promoter)** framework, this module emphasizes **Growth** and **Theme** most heavily across all five lectures, while integrating **Promoter** and **Valuation** as essential verification and sizing filters:
- **Growth:** Quantified via earnings breakouts (>20–30% PAT/sales growth), operating profit margin (OPM) expansion, positive earnings surprises, and the return equation (`Perception × Earnings Growth × Rate of Change of Perception`) (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05).
- **Theme:** Anchored by industry-first Stage 2 leadership ("mother and kittens"), S-curve adoption (>10% share), horizontal value-chain mapping (e.g., global AI data-center bottlenecks), and B2B/B2G capex cycles (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec03, FAS-Lec04).
- **Promoter:** Screened through promoter warrant issuances (25% upfront, 18-month lock-in), promoter stake increases (>2%), M&A/capex disclosures, and auditor/SEBI red-flag checks (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05).
- **Valuation:** Framed forward-looking via a J-curve mindset, the 15–30x P/E rerating sweet spot (<40x), PEG < 1.5x for global AI/GARP screens, and forward FCF multiples—explicitly warning against backward-looking TTM P/E and valuation anchoring (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec03, FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05).

## 3. CORE PILLARS OF THE MODULE

### Pillar 1: The "NEW" Catalyst & J-Curve Growth Mindset
- **Why it matters:** Stock returns follow `Perception × Earnings Growth × Rate of Change of Perception` (FAS-Lec01). Relying on static "buy quality and do nothing" strategies or trailing-12-month (TTM) P/E multiples causes investors to miss emerging J-curves and hold stagnating leaders (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec04).
- **Progression across lectures:** FAS-Lec01 introduces the 14 "NEW" catalyst triggers and S-curve/value-chain mapping → FAS-Lec02 operationalizes them into automated Indian earnings/OPM/announcement scans → FAS-Lec04 codifies them into free Screener.in CANSLIM "C" queries focused on B2B/B2G capex cycles.
- **Key rules/thresholds:** >20–25% actual or guided growth (100–150 StockScans one-pagers/quarter) (FAS-Lec01); >10% S-curve adoption inflection (FAS-Lec01); Sales YoY > 15%, PAT YoY > 20%, ROCE > 15%, P/E 15–30x (<40x) with a 5–6Q runway (FAS-Lec04).
- **Read more:** FAS-Lec01 (Stages 1–6, 9–10), FAS-Lec02 (Stages 4, 8–9), FAS-Lec04 (Stages 1–4).

### Pillar 2: Industry-First Stage 2 & Relative Strength ("Mother and Kittens")
- **Why it matters:** Sector (12%) and industry group (37%) account for ~49% of a stock's price move (and 60%+ in group moves) (FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04). Identifying Stage 2 industry tailwinds prevents buying isolated laggards or stocks fighting sector headwinds (FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04).
- **Progression across lectures:** FAS-Lec02 introduces custom industry indices (MCap > ₹1,000 cr) and P2P peer comparison → FAS-Lec03 extends Stage 2 technical scanning globally via TradingView, IBD 50, and Perplexity RS rankings → FAS-Lec04 formalizes 26-week Comparative RS vs. Nifty 50 (Levy 1967 benchmark) and 21-day StockEdge RS for hotel/sector group moves.
- **Key rules/thresholds:** Top 10% 26W RS benchmark of 9.6%/26W (~20.1% p.a.) vs. 2.9%/26W (FAS-Lec04); StockScans weekly RS > 2% and < 10% vs. Nifty 50 (FAS-Lec04); TradingView global scan: Close ≥ 30W EMA, ADX > 20, Weekly RSI > 50 (FAS-Lec03).
- **Read more:** FAS-Lec02 (Stages 1–3, 13), FAS-Lec03 (Stages 2–4), FAS-Lec04 (Stages 6, 8–9).

### Pillar 3: Techno-Fundamental Convergence & Price-Volume Discipline
- **Why it matters:** Screening without an investing framework leads to buying Stage 4 falling knives or holding Stage 3 tops through silent P/E expansion (FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05). Requiring technical confirmation alongside fundamental catalysts filters out false positives and PE exit overhangs (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05).
- **Progression across lectures:** FAS-Lec01 introduces **Scan Match** (intersecting fundamental scans with technical breakouts) → FAS-Lec02 pairs Fundamental Earnings Breakouts with 200–1,000D/40–200D technical breakouts and VSTOP → FAS-Lec04 adds Volume Rocketing (10D vol ≥ 1.5x 20D vol) and absolute delivered shares → FAS-Lec05 anchors the entire pipeline in Weinstein's 30W/40W EMA, O'Neil's price-volume matrix, tightening patterns, and explicit Stage 3/4 exit rules.
- **Key rules/thresholds:** Close > rising 30W/40W EMA for Stage 2 entry; exit half in Stage 3 after 4–5 bases and full on close < 30W MA (FAS-Lec05); 10D Vol > 1.20x 50D Vol (FAS-Lec02) or ≥ 1.5x 20D Vol (FAS-Lec04); tightening pattern of 3–5 weeks to 2–3 months on drying volume (FAS-Lec05). Note: FAS-Lec02 uses 1.20x (10D vs. 50D Vol) in its combined scan, whereas FAS-Lec04 uses ≥ 1.5x (10D vs. 20D Vol) for Volume Rocketing.
- **Read more:** FAS-Lec01 (Stage 10), FAS-Lec02 (Stages 4–5, 13), FAS-Lec04 (Stages 5, 8–9), FAS-Lec05 (Stages 1–6).

### Pillar 4: Qualitative Filing, Concall & Institutional Sponsorship Verification
- **Why it matters:** Quantitative screens only show *what* moved, never *why* ("StockScans, not StockBuy") (FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec03). Hidden disclosures inside PPTs, concalls, auditor notes, and institutional/promoter flows separate durable compounders from accounting traps and overhead supply (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04).
- **Progression across lectures:** FAS-Lec01 establishes real-time order/capex alerts, warrant backtests, and annual report auditor red-flag scans → FAS-Lec02 adds Full Search inside PDFs/PPTs, AI concall decoding, and FII+DII backtests → FAS-Lec03 translates this workflow to Perplexity Finance live-translated concalls and 6–7 hrs/day Substack/X reading → FAS-Lec04 adds Google Alerts on marquee investors and PE overhang detection.
- **Key rules/thresholds:** Promoter warrants (25% upfront, 18-mo lock-in: 72.4% 3Y / 77.8% 5Y hit rate) (FAS-Lec01); FII+DII 1Y Δ ≥ 2% (~46% 1Y outperformance rate alone, requiring Stage 2 confirmation) (FAS-Lec02) or FII+DII 1Y > 1–3% / QoQ > 0.5% + 3M RS > 2% (FAS-Lec04).
- **Read more:** FAS-Lec01 (Stages 8, 11), FAS-Lec02 (Stages 6–7, 10–12), FAS-Lec03 (Stages 5, 7), FAS-Lec04 (Stages 3–4, 7, 10).

## 5. MASTER FRAMEWORK / CHECKLIST

1. **Lock In Your Investing Framework & Portfolio Split:** Choose your primary style (techno-fundamental, GARP, momentum, or deep value) before screening (FAS-Lec05). Allocate 70–80% of capital to active industry trends and 20–30% to contrarian bets (FAS-Lec01), and set global equity exposure at 10–20% alongside 80–85% Indian equities (FAS-Lec03).
2. **Identify Stage 2 Industry Tailwinds ("Mother and Kittens"):** Build custom industry indices (MCap > ₹1,000 cr) above EMA/VSTOP and <20% below 52W highs (FAS-Lec02), or check StockScans sectoral heatmaps and 21-day StockEdge RS to spot group moves where 60%+ of a stock's move comes from its sector/industry (FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04).
3. **Run Quantitative Catalyst & Stage 2 Scans:**
   - *Domestic (StockScans / Screener.in / MarketSmith):* Filter for Earnings Breakouts (MCap ₹1,000–20,000 cr, prior 4Q PAT within 10% band, latest PAT > 30%), Consistent OPM expansion (OPM(t) > OPM(t-1) × 1.10, OPM > 1%), CANSLIM "C" (Sales > 15%, PAT > 20%, ROCE > 15%, P/E 15–30x / < 40), or *Scan Match* combining *Buoyant Growth* (>20% Sales & PAT) with technical breakouts (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04).
   - *International (TradingView / IBD 50 / Perplexity Max):* Filter weekly charts for Close ≥ 30W EMA, ADX > 20, and Weekly RSI > 50, check IBD 50 (\$10/mo), or run automated Perplexity Max RS + earnings-upgrade scans across ~1,200 US/European tickers (FAS-Lec03).
4. **Verify Price-Volume & Relative Strength Confirmation:** Confirm Stage 2 positioning above a rising 30W/40W EMA, 26-week RS > 2% and < 10% vs. Nifty 50 (targeting top-decile RS), and institutional volume expansion (10D vol ≥ 1.5x 20D vol or > 1.20x 50D vol) with rising absolute delivered shares or a 3–5 week to 2–3 month tightening pattern (FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05).
5. **Execute Qualitative Deep Dive & Red-Flag Audit:** Never buy a raw scan hit ("StockScans, not StockBuy") (FAS-Lec02). Run Full Search inside PDFs/PPTs for "NEW" catalysts (capex, M&A, warrants, geography, regulation), decode concalls (via StockScans AI prompts or Perplexity Finance), check FII+DII/promoter accumulation vs. PE exit overhangs, and screen annual reports for auditor disclaimers or SEBI notices (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec03, FAS-Lec04).
6. **Run P2P Peer Selection & Forward Modeling:** Compare up to 10 industry peers on 1Y RS, PAT growth, YoY revenue, and EBITDA margin to select the strongest earnings leader (FAS-Lec02), then build forward bull/base/bear models checking theme expiry dates (FAS-Lec01).
7. **Size Positions & Enforce Objective Exits:** Spread capital across 20–25 stocks using **2-4-6-8 sizing** (2% low, 4% OK, 6% good, 8% high conviction) (FAS-Lec01). After 4–5 bases, sell half in Stage 3 and exit fully on a weekly close below the 30-week MA to avoid Stage 4 drawdowns (FAS-Lec05).

## 8. COMMON MISTAKES & RED FLAGS ACROSS THE MODULE

- **Treating Scans as Buy Signals ("StockScans, not StockBuy"):** Buying raw screen hits without reading concalls, checking pre-sales/churn, or verifying industry Stage 2 strength leads to value traps and false positives (FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec03, FAS-Lec04).
- **TTM P/E & Valuation Anchoring Traps:** Chasing 8–9x P/E sunset industries (e.g., print, cigarettes) or refusing to buy Stage 2 breakouts after an initial move kills compounding (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec05).
- **Confusing Stage 4 Bounces with Stage 1 Bases:** Price below a declining 30W EMA carries heavy overhead supply from trapped buyers and risks immediate 10–20% drawdowns (FAS-Lec04, FAS-Lec05).
- **Misreading Institutional & Warrant Signals:** FII+DII stake increases (~46% 1Y hit rate) and warrant issuances fail without Stage 2 industry tailwinds; conversely, PE exit overhangs can stall cheap stocks for years (FAS-Lec01, FAS-Lec02, FAS-Lec04).
- **Superficial Filing & Delivery Checks:** Relying on Quick Search misses critical capex/merger details buried inside PPTs (FAS-Lec02), while rejecting high-volume breakouts over lower delivery % ignores surging absolute delivered shares (FAS-Lec04).
- **Accounting, Cyclical & Governance Blind Spots:** Labeling cyclical Stage 2 winners as "consistent compounders" due to buybacks/dividends (FAS-Lec05), ignoring auditor disclaimers/SEBI notices (FAS-Lec01), or overlooking theme saturation and regulatory shocks (FAS-Lec01).
- **Over-Relying on Unverified AI Outputs:** Treating initial AI prompts as final instead of iterating/backtesting, or skipping deep reading (6–7 hrs/day on Substacks and filings) (FAS-Lec03).
