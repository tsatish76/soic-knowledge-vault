## FAS-Lec01: Identifying Growth Catalysts with StockScans (Level 5: How to Screen & Filter Epic Stocks)

Duration: 1:39:09 | TVGP focus: Growth, Theme, Promoter, Valuation
Detailed report: FAS_Lec01_Report.md

### 1. Core thesis
Stock returns are governed by Perception × Earnings Growth × Rate of Change of Perception (where P/E = Perception ÷ Earnings), making static "buy quality and do nothing" strategies vulnerable when market regimes shift. Active investors must abandon backward-looking trailing-12-month (TTM) P/E obsession for a probabilistic **J-curve mindset**—systematically hunting the 14 "NEW" business catalysts via StockScans before earnings materialize.

### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **70–30 / 80–20 Portfolio Split:** Align 70–80% of holdings (6–7 of 10 stocks) with active industry trends and reserve 20–30% for contrarian bets; avoid catching falling knives in stagnant quality names → FAS-Lec01 – Stage 1.
- **14 "NEW" Catalyst Triggers:** Screen for new base creation, industry cycles, management/promoter changes, corporate actions, capex, new-age models, geographies, regulations, value-added products, verticals, acquisitions, warrant issues, and deleveraging → FAS-Lec01 – Stage 3.
- **Horizontal S-Curve & Value-Chain Mapping:** Spot >10% adoption inflections and global bottlenecks (e.g., 5-year US data-center grid delays) and trace component/ecosystem beneficiaries down to Indian listed suppliers → FAS-Lec01 – Stage 4, Stage 5.
- **Quarterly Growth Catalyst Habit:** Filter companies growing/guiding >20–25%, read 100–150 StockScans one-pagers post-results, cross-check technical Stage Analysis, and verify theme expiry dates → FAS-Lec01 – Stage 6.
- **Scan Match & Announcement Automation:** Intersect fundamental scans (*Buoyant Growth*, *Explosive Growth*, *Margin Mavericks*, *Driving Deleverage*) with technical breakouts in **Scan Match**, paired with real-time Telegram order/capex alerts and Annual Report auditor red-flag scans → FAS-Lec01 – Stage 10, Stage 11.
- **2-4-6-8 Portfolio Sizing:** Spread capital across 20–25 stocks at 2% (low), 4% (OK), 6% (good), and 8% (high conviction) weights → FAS-Lec01 – Stage 12.

### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas
| Rule/Formula | Value/Condition | Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Return Equation | Perception × Earnings Growth × Rate of Change | Stage 2 |
| Exponential Compounding | Prior top line (1–3 yrs ago) ≈ Today's bottom line | Stage 3 |

| Catalyst Screen Universe | >20–25% actual or guided growth (100–150/qtr) | Stage 6 |
| Promoter Warrant Backtest | 25% upfront, 18-mo lock-in; 72.4% 3Y / 77.8% 5Y hit rate | Stage 8 |
| Buoyant Growth Scan | >20% Sales growth AND >20% PAT growth | Stage 10 |
| 2-4-6-8 Sizing | 20–25 stocks; 2% / 4% / 6% / 8% weights | Stage 12 |

### 5. Cautions & red flags
- **TTM P/E & Price Anchoring Traps:** Chasing 8–9x P/E sunset industries (print, cigarettes) or skipping multi-year winners after early moves kills compounding [0:18:18, 0:23:18].
- **Theme Expiry & Regulatory Shock:** Every S-curve saturates (EV 3W at 60% share), while RBI prop-trading norms cut MCX options ADT ~40% [0:43:12, 0:44:00].

### 6. Decisive takeaway
Drop backward-looking accounting/TTM P/E habits and anchor every research workflow to public "NEW" catalysts—combining StockScans (*Buoyant Growth*, *Scan Match*, and real-time announcement/auditor scans) with value-chain mapping and forward bull/base/bear modeling. Compounding requires **Conscientiousness** and **DCP** (Discipline, Consistency, Patience) within a 2-4-6-8 sized portfolio.

## FAS-Lec02: Screening Domestic Businesses: Relative Strength, Earnings Breakouts, Margin Expansion & Qualitative Scans

Duration: 1:23:41 | TVGP focus: Growth, Theme, Promoter
Detailed report: FAS_Lec02_Report.md

### 1. Core thesis
Manually screening ~5,000 listed Indian companies takes roughly four months; automated scanners (StockScans) compress discovery to minutes so investor effort shifts to reading filings and concalls. Combining industry-level Stage 2 Relative Strength ("mother and kittens") with quantitative earnings/margin breakouts and qualitative announcement scans surfaces high-probability leaders early—even when broad indices remain range-bound.

### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **Industry-First Stage 2 (Mother & Kittens):** Sector (12%) and industry group (37%) drive ~49% of a stock's move; build custom indices (MCap > ₹1,000 cr) from stocks above EMA/VSTOP and <20% below 52W highs to catch Stage 1 → Stage 2 industry inflections → FAS-Lec02 – Stage 1 & 3.
- **5,000-to-Watchlist Funnel:** Universe → Scan → Verify & Research → Watchlist ("StockScans, not StockBuy"); distinguish RSI (internal momentum) from RS (leadership vs. peers/benchmark) → FAS-Lec02 – Stage 2.
- **3-Step Breakout & AI Concall Decoding:** Pair Fundamental Earnings Breakout with Technical Breakout (200–1,000D Long-Term or 40–200D Short-Term, Close > 30W EMA, positive VSTOP), then decode concalls using StockScans AI prompts → FAS-Lec02 – Stage 4, 5 & 7.
- **Qualitative Announcement Full Search:** Scan inside PDFs/PPTs (not just titles) for warrant issues, M&A (@Company + acquisition), marquee concall questions, and BSE >1% shareholding disclosures → FAS-Lec02 – Stage 6, 11 & 12.
- **P2P Peer Selection:** Compare up to 10 industry peers on 1Y RS, PAT growth, YoY revenue, and EBITDA margin to pick the strongest earnings leader → FAS-Lec02 – Stage 13.

### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas
| Rule / Filter | Value / Condition | Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Earnings Breakout Scan | MCap ₹1,000–20,000 cr; prior 4Q PAT within 1.10 (10%) band; latest PAT > 30% (320 → 13 stocks) | Stage 4 |
| Consistent OPM Scan (3–4Q) | MCap ≥ ₹1,000 cr; OPM(t) > OPM(t-1) × 1.10; latest OPM > 1% (excludes banks) | Stage 8 |
| Result Tracker Scan (YoY) | Margin expansion > 20%; sales growth > 10%; latest OPM > 6% | Stage 8 |
| Consistent 4Q PAT Growth | PAT growth > 10% for 4 straight quarters (PAT preferred over EPS) | Stage 9 |
| Loss-to-Profit IPO Scan | Listed < 600 days; PAT 1Q back < 0; PAT YoY Q < 0; latest PAT > ₹1 cr | Stage 9 |
| Institutional Interest (FII+DII) | MCap ≥ ₹1,000–2,500 cr; FII+DII Δ ≥ 2% (1Y backtest: 25/54 outperformed, ~46%) | Stage 10 |
| Combined Volume + Earnings | Close > 30W EMA; 10D Vol > 1.20 × 50D Vol; RS > 2%; PAT > 20% (5,000 → 236 → 83) | Stage 13 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Shipping (+50%) vs. Footwear (-37%):** Proved industry Stage 2 tailwinds lift constituents while weak sectors drag them down → Stage 2 [0:08:51–0:10:00].
- **Usha Martin & Kirloskar Pneumatic:** Earnings/OPM breakouts explained by concalls—Usha Martin targeting 20%+ EBITDA margin and 20.6% ROCE; Kirloskar Pneumatic expanding OPM from 11% to 26% via 128 IPs → Stage 7 & 8 [0:35:34–0:44:17].
- **PB FinTech & CarTrade Tech:** PB FinTech OPM rose ~2.5% → ~10% (PAT ₹72 → ₹261 cr); CarTrade (27.2% → ~35% OPM) showed why YoY Result Tracker complements strict QoQ filters → Stage 8 [0:43:29–0:45:45].
- **Shaily Engineering vs. PCBL:** Both had rising FII/DII stakes, yet Shaily rallied in Stage 2 while PCBL fell into Stage 4 amid weak pricing → Stage 10 [0:57:46–0:59:27].
- **HFCL (₹555 cr warrants) & Sequent–Viyash Merger:** Full Search exposed capex vs. working capital split inside PPTs and 56:100 swap ratio synergies → Stage 6 & 12 [0:27:55, 1:16:18–1:17:31].

### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Never treat a scan as a buy signal ("StockScans, not StockBuy"); verify filings, valuation, and industry stage.
- Warrant issues and FII+DII stake increases (~46% 1Y hit rate) fail without Stage 2 industry tailwinds.
- Always use OPM > 1% to exclude banks and PAT YoY Q < 0 on IPO turnarounds to avoid seasonal false positives.
- Relying on Quick Search misses disclosures buried inside attached PPTs; run Full Search.

### 6. Decisive takeaway
Screen for industry-level Stage 2 strength first, then require convergence across earnings/margin breakouts, volume-backed price action, and qualitative concall or filing catalysts before adding a stock to your watchlist. Always finish with a P2P peer check so you own the strongest earnings leader in the group.

## FAS-Lec04: Tools to Find Epic Stocks: CANSLIM, Screener, MarketSmith, StockScans, Relative Strength & Google Alerts

Duration: 02:10:14 | TVGP focus: Theme, Growth, Valuation, Promoter
Detailed report: FAS_Lec04_Report.md

### 1. Core thesis
Filtering 5,000+ listed Indian stocks requires systematic tools that surface businesses undergoing a fundamental rate of change—anchored by something **NEW** (product, capex, geography, client, or regulation). Combining quantitative earnings screens, volume/institutional sponsorship checks, and 26-week Relative Strength isolates Stage 2 sector leaders early while avoiding deep-value traps and PE-overhang laggards.

### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **"NEW" Catalyst & B2B/B2G Focus:** Hunt multi-year rate-of-change triggers in B2B/B2G capex cycles (3–5+ years) rather than >95% penetrated B2C staples → FAS-Lec04 – Stage 1 & 2.
- **Free Screener.in CANSLIM "C" Query:** Filter Sales YoY > 15%, PAT YoY > 20%, ROCE > 15%, MCap > ₹1,000 cr, and P/E < 40 (15–30x rerating sweet spot), then read concalls for "NEW" catalysts → FAS-Lec04 – Stage 3 & 4.
- **Volume Rocketing & Delivery Check:** Screen 10D avg vol ≥ 1.5x 20D avg vol for institutional accumulation; track absolute delivered shares rather than delivery % → FAS-Lec04 – Stage 5.
- **MarketSmith Two-Screen Playbook:** Check *Growth 50* (Recent Additions + Removals) and *Blue Dot* (52W RS high) weekly using a 1–1.5% AUM learning budget → FAS-Lec04 – Stage 6.
- **Institutional & Promoter Screens:** Screen 1Y FII+DII > 1–3%, Promoter > 2%, or QoQ FII/DII > 0.5% + 3M RS > 2% to avoid PE exit supply → FAS-Lec04 – Stage 7.
- **Weinstein Stage Analysis & 26W RS:** Pair 8 Variant Perception triggers with 30W EMA, price-volume action, and 26-week Comparative RS vs. Nifty 50 (plus 21-day StockEdge RS for group moves) → FAS-Lec04 – Stage 8 & 9.
- **Free Cheat Codes:** Set Google Alerts on marquee investors and monitor StockScans green sectoral return heatmaps → FAS-Lec04 – Stage 10.

### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas
| Rule/Formula | Value/Condition | Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Indian CANSLIM "C" | Sales > 15%, PAT > 20%, P/E 15–30x (< 40), 5–6Q runway | Stage 2–3 |
| Volume Rocketing | 10D avg vol ≥ 1.5x 20D avg vol; avoid < ₹300 cr MCap | Stage 5 |
| Industry/Sector Attribution | 37% industry group + 12% sector (~49%; 60%+ group moves) | Stage 6 |

| Levy 1967 RS Benchmark | Top 10% 26W RS: 9.6%/26W (~20.1% p.a.) vs. 2.9%/26W | Stage 8 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Shaily Engg, HBL Power & Apar Industries** → "NEW" EpiPens, Kavach, and AL-59 conductors (margins 5–6% → 8–10%) drove multi-year breakouts → Stage 1, 4, 5 [0:04:11, 0:48:44, 0:54:18].

### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Rejecting high-volume breakouts over lower delivery % when absolute delivered shares surge 4x.
- Buying deep-value or PE-overhang stocks without growth catalysts or Stage 2 confirmation.
- Holding Stage 4 or contracting-earnings names (e.g., SRF) through silent P/E expansion.
- Treating screen hits as buy signals without checking concalls, pre-sales, or churn.

### 6. Decisive takeaway
Pair free quantitative screens (CANSLIM growth, FII/DII accumulation, and 26-week/21-day Relative Strength) with systematic concall reading to isolate businesses undergoing a genuine "NEW" catalyst in leading industry groups. Never buy a cheap multiple or a screen hit until price-volume action and management commentary confirm Stage 2 momentum.

## FAS-Lec05: Learn Stage Analysis to Avoid Falling Knives: Price-Volume Behaviour & Techno-Fundamental Framework

Duration: 00:27:13 | TVGP focus: Growth, Theme, Promoter
Detailed report: FAS_Lec05_Report.md

### 1. Core thesis
Stock filtration fails without a defined investing framework first. Combining Stan Weinstein's 4-stage weekly chart structure (30/40-week EMA) and William O'Neil's price-volume rules with earnings-surprise fundamentals lets investors enter Stage 2 institutional markups early while avoiding Stage 4 "falling knives" and overhead supply.

### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **SOIC Stock-Picking Pipeline:** Define Framework (techno-fundamental, GARP, deep value, or momentum) → Filter Stocks → Study & Checklist → SOIC Ranking Sheet → Stock Picking → FAS-Lec05 – Stage 1 [00:01:11–00:03:33].
- **O'Neil Price-Volume Matrix:** Rising price + rising volume signals bullish markup (Scenario 1); falling price + rising volume signals dumping; extreme downtrend + rising volume signals fundamental change to investigate (Scenario 3) → FAS-Lec05 – Stage 2 [00:04:05–00:06:34].
- **Tightening Pattern:** After a strong rally, a narrow sideways range on drying volume for 3–5 weeks to 2–3 months indicates missing selling force ahead of the next fundamental breakout → FAS-Lec05 – Stage 3 [00:06:34–00:09:55].
- **4-Stage Earnings Surprise Cycle:** Ignored Stage 1 ("cricket pitch", 2–3 yrs) transitions to Stage 2 on positive PAT beats + industry tailwinds, tops in Stage 3, and collapses into Stage 4 on missed guidance and downward EPS revisions → FAS-Lec05 – Stage 4 [00:09:55–00:13:06].
- **Weinstein Basing & 30W/40W EMA Rules:** Long horizontal bases transfer weak hands to strong hands during 2–3 yrs of hidden capex; buy at Stage 2 start/pullback and exit on Stage 4 close below 30W MA → FAS-Lec05 – Stage 5 & Stage 6 [00:13:06–00:26:44].

### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas
| Rule / Formula | Value / Condition | Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tightening Pattern | Narrow range, very low volume for 3–5 wks / 2–3 mos | Stage 3 |
| Weekly Trend Filter | Price > rising 30W/40W EMA (Stage 2); < declining EMA (Stage 4) | Stage 5 |
| Institutional Volume | High weekly volume spikes in ₹1,800–4,000 cr MCap stocks | Stage 5 |
| Low-Float Watchlist | ₹6,000–7,000 cr MCap, ₹10–20 cr/day turnover + post-Stage 4 volume | Stage 5 |
| Stage 1 Hidden Capex | 2–3 yrs for new products/capex to reflect in numbers | Stage 6 |
| Stage 4 Buy Risk | Immediate 10–20% drawdowns when buying Stage 4 | Stage 6 |
| Stage 3 / 4 Exit Rule | After 4–5 bases: sell half in Stage 3, full on close < 30W MA | Stage 6 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Pulak Prasad / Thaero Chaos [?]** → Framework dictated selling immediately when promoter/management changed → Stage 1 [00:01:11–00:02:33].
- **2018 Banks & Finance** → Ignored Stage 1 "cricket pitch" for 2–3 yrs before becoming Stage 2 leaders → Stage 4 [00:10:27–00:11:04].

### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Never mistake price below a declining 30W EMA for Stage 1; trapped buyers create heavy overhead supply on every bounce [00:18:01–00:19:14].
- Do not label cyclical Stage 2 winners as "consistent compounders" even with buybacks/dividends [00:19:14–00:20:23].
- Avoid buying Stage 4 falling knives (risking 10–20% immediate drops) and avoid valuation anchoring that misses Stage 2 breakouts [00:23:04–00:24:37].

### 6. Decisive takeaway
Establish your techno-fundamental framework before running screens, then restrict entries to Stage 2 breakouts or pullbacks above a rising 30-week EMA backed by institutional volume and positive earnings surprises. Respect an objective close below the 30-week moving average (or half in Stage 3 after 4–5 bases) to exit before Stage 4 capital erosion.
