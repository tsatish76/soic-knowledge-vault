## FAS-Lec03 – Stage 2: Free Technical Screening on TradingView (Stage 2 Momentum Scan) [0:01:08–0:04:53] [TVGP: Growth]

### a) What this stage answers
How can an investor build a completely free technical Stage 2 momentum screener on TradingView to spot international stocks entering an emerging uptrend?

### b) Detailed explanation
To begin, search for **"TradingView screener"** on Google and open the TradingView Stock Screener [0:01:08–0:01:39] [Slide 52]. Under the **Country of origin** filter, you can screen the entire world or narrow down to a specific market such as the **USA**, **Germany**, or **Japan** [0:01:08–0:01:39], [0:02:33–0:03:09].

#### Applying the SOIC Stage Analysis Framework
Using the Stage Analysis concepts taught earlier in the SOIC course (ADX > 20, Relative Strength, RSI, and the 30-week moving average), apply three momentum indicators on **weekly charts** [0:01:39–0:04:22]:

1. **Average Directional Index (ADX) — Emerging Trend:** Under Technicals, select **ADX** and filter for **ADX crossing up 20** (above 20) [0:02:00–0:02:33]. ADX measures how strongly a stock price is moving in a trend; the moment ADX crosses above 20, the stock is entering an **emerging trend** [0:02:00–0:02:33].
2. **Relative Strength Index (RSI) — Fresh Momentum:** Filter for **Weekly RSI just crossed above 50** [0:02:00–0:02:33]. The moment RSI crosses the 50 mark on weekly charts, the stock is entering **fresh momentum** [0:02:33–0:03:09].
3. **30-Week Exponential Moving Average (EMA) — Stage 2 Confirmation:** Under Technicals, go to Exponential Moving Average, perform a manual setup with **Length = 30** on **Weekly charts**, and set the condition to **Close ≥ 30-week EMA** (stocks trading at or above their 30-week EMA, or just crossing above it) [0:03:43–0:04:22].

You can also view **Analyst Ratings** (e.g., Neutral or Buy ratings) directly in the TradingView screener output alongside these technicals to see what sell-side analysts are showing [0:01:39–0:02:00], [0:02:33–0:03:43].

**Speaker's view:** All three indicators (Close ≥ 30-week EMA, Weekly RSI > 50, ADX > 20) are momentum indicators that flag businesses just entering **Stage 2** [0:04:22–0:04:53]. When a stock shows up on this technical scan, it typically indicates that **some sort of earnings upgrade has started happening in the business** [0:04:22–0:04:53].
