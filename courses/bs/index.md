# BS – Sub-Course Master Summary: Buy Sell (`06_L4_When_To_BuySell`)
## 1. OVERARCHING THEME & OBJECTIVE
The **Buy Sell (BS)** module builds an end-to-end, probability-based decision system for timing **entries, pyramiding, sector rotation, and exits** in Indian equities (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec07, BS-Lec12). Rather than relying on subjective valuation targets or price anchoring, learners combine fundamental inflection triggers with Weinstein Stage Analysis, Moving Averages (10W/21W/30W EMA), Volatility Stop (V-Stop), ADX, Comparative Relative Strength (CRS), Volume/OBV, and custom/ratio sector charts to ride Stage 2 compounding while systematically exiting before Stage 4 drawdowns (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec04, BS-Lec08, BS-Lec12).

Within the **TVGP** framework, the module focuses most deeply on **Valuation** (GARP PEG < 1.5x, Value PEG < 1x, P/B < 1x, Reverse DCF, P/E compression, and avoiding 100–300x P/E or peak-margin traps) and **Growth** (10 NOS triggers, ≥ +5% analyst estimate upgrades/PEAD, Pre-Opex CWIP > 50%, order-book cycles, convexity, and structural mix shifts) (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec07, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12). **Theme** is developed through top-down sector rotation (67% group move rule, custom equi-weighted indices, ratio charts, RRG, and Corporate Profit/GDP) (BS-Lec08), while **Promoter** is addressed via open-market insider buying triangulation with >20-MA volume, promoter selling flags, and hard governance exits for pledging or auditor/CFO resignations (BS-Lec06, BS-Lec08, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec12).

## 3. CORE PILLARS OF THE MODULE
### Pillar 1: Stage Analysis & Volatility Stop (V-Stop) Discipline
- **Why it matters:** Asymmetric loss math (-50% requires +100% to recover) means avoiding Stage 4 breakdowns is paramount (BS-Lec01). Subjective valuation targets fail during multi-year momentum runs, whereas V-Stop (`ATR(14) × Multiplier`, Length = 10) provides an objective trailing exit (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec12).
- **Progression across lectures:** BS-Lec01 (introduced Monthly V-Stop 2.5x core / 1.5–2x cyclical & Stage 1–4) → BS-Lec02 (paired with ADX > 20/25; Weekly V-Stop 1.5–2.0 for 6–9 mo cyclicals) → BS-Lec04 (2.0x cyclical vs. 2.5x compounder multipliers) → BS-Lec05 (30W EMA + Weekly V-Stop 2.0x for satellite portfolios) → BS-Lec09 (automated in SOIC LTI & Stage Indicator) → BS-Lec12 (3-trigger weekly exit: 30W EMA + 26W RS + V-Stop(10, 2)).
- **Key rules/thresholds:** Length = 10; Multiplier = 2.5–3.0x Monthly for core compounders, 1.5–2.0x Weekly for cyclicals/100x P/E stocks; accept 10%–25% giveback on weekly exits (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec02, BS-Lec04, BS-Lec12).
- **Read more:** BS-Lec01 (Stages 5, 8, 10), BS-Lec02 (Stages 3–5), BS-Lec04 (Stage 4), BS-Lec05 (Stage 4), BS-Lec09 (Stages 2–4), BS-Lec12 (Stage 5).
### Pillar 2: Relative Strength, Volume & Multi-Indicator Confluence
- **Why it matters:** Cheap value stocks can stay dead money for years without institutional liquidity (BS-Lec03, BS-Lec08). Pairing Comparative Relative Strength (CRS) with expanding volume (> 20-MA, OBV slope) and Weekly RSI confirms smart-money accumulation before committing full capital (BS-Lec03, BS-Lec06).
- **Progression across lectures:** BS-Lec01 (Weekly RSI >83–85 block, RSI 50 support, CRS) → BS-Lec03 (CRS + V-Stop + volume; StockEdge 55-day RS > 0 funnel) → BS-Lec04 (Parabolic SAR 0.04/0.05 pyramiding + Weekly RSI > 50 + OBV) → BS-Lec06 (20-MA Volume, lifetime-high bars, OBV slope asymmetry) → BS-Lec09 (SOIC LTI ADX = 0 lag fix + RSI 45 + RS 52/12) → BS-Lec11 (Stock Scans as 70–80% screening filter).
- **Key rules/thresholds:** Institutional mcap floor > ₹400–500 Cr (BS-Lec01); Volume > 20-MA + upward OBV slope for Stage 2 breakouts, never wait for OBV to turn down to sell (BS-Lec06); disable ADX lag (`ADX = 0`) in SOIC LTI when RSI 45 + RS + V-Stop already align (BS-Lec09).
- **Read more:** BS-Lec01 (Stages 6–9), BS-Lec03 (Stages 2–5), BS-Lec04 (Stages 3, 6), BS-Lec06 (Stages 1–5), BS-Lec09 (Stages 4–6).
### Pillar 3: Top-Down Sector Rotation & Market Regime Breadth
- **Why it matters:** Per William O'Neil's study, 67% (2 of 3) of major winners move in synchronized industry packs; even strong leaders like SRF and PI Industries suffer multi-year time corrections inside weak sectors (BS-Lec08). Market environment drives 60%–70% of technical/techno-funda outcomes (BS-Lec10).
- **Progression across lectures:** BS-Lec03 (peer-to-peer CRS) → BS-Lec08 (67% group move rule, bracketed equi-weighted custom indices, ratio charts + 30W EMA alerts, Corporate Profit/GDP, 10W/3–6M RRG, Chartink breadth) → BS-Lec11 (regime-matched entries: horizontal BO in bull, bottom reversal in bear, pullbacks/21 DMA in sideways) → BS-Lec12 (Nifty P/E 19–20x & 10–20% panic breadth redeployment).
- **Key rules/thresholds:** >70% sector stocks beating index = bull run; Chartink % > 200 EMA at 12%–15% = panic SIP zone; % > 20 EMA < 15% + Nifty Forward P/E < 20x = lump-sum signal (BS-Lec08); 19x–20x Nifty P/E & 10%–20% panic breadth (BS-Lec12).
- **Read more:** BS-Lec08 (Stages 2–11), BS-Lec10 (Stage 2), BS-Lec11 (Stages 6, 8), BS-Lec12 (Stages 1, 7).
### Pillar 4: Fundamental Entry Archetypes, Inflection Triggers & Inversion
- **Why it matters:** Technicals alone catch governance traps (BCG, Gensol), while fundamentals alone suffer value traps (Karnataka Bank) or peak-margin/peak-order-book collapses (GMM Pfaudler) (BS-Lec07, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12). Defining your archetype (Value, GARP, Momentum, Special Situation, Pessimism) aligns entry criteria, sizing, and time horizon (BS-Lec07, BS-Lec10).
- **Progression across lectures:** BS-Lec07 (inversion "when NOT to buy", 5 archetypes, 10 NOS triggers, ≥ +5% PEAD revisions, Most Lethal Combo) → BS-Lec10 (Bombed-out IPOs 15–24 mo, Speed Breaker vs. *Khai*, Purple Patch, Special Situations/Self-Help, Pessimism, Value + 4–8 qtr time stop) → BS-Lec11 (Strategy-first Pre-Opex CWIP > 50%, Order-Book cycle, Convexity, Boiling Frog mix shift, Young Momentum).
- **Key rules/thresholds:** GARP PEG < 1.5x, Value PEG < 1.0x or P/B < 1.0x or >6%–7% yield; Bombed-out IPO 15–24 mo (or < 500 days scan); Speed Breaker sizing 2%–4%–6%–8%; Pre-Opex CWIP > 50% of fixed assets; 4–8 quarter time stop-loss (BS-Lec07, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12).
- **Read more:** BS-Lec07 (Stages 2–7, 11), BS-Lec10 (Stages 2–10), BS-Lec11 (Stages 1–7).
### Pillar 5: Systematic Exits, Position Sizing & Behavioral Discipline
- **Why it matters:** Most investors enter without pre-defined exit triggers, anchor to break-even prices, or hold 80–90 stocks, destroying capital in Stage 4 downtrends (BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12). Written 4-sentence buy theses, overweight trims, and emotionless weekly exits preserve capital for redeployment (BS-Lec12).
- **Progression across lectures:** BS-Lec04 (2–4 tranches max for 3%–6% allocation) → BS-Lec07 (Munger inversion & avoiding horizon mismatch) → BS-Lec10 (4–8 qtr value time stop-loss) → BS-Lec11 (15–25 stock cap, 10W/21W EMA momentum trailing, 30–40 min/day Banyan Tree reading, 3–5 yr MF test) → BS-Lec12 (Exit *Chakravyuh*, 4-sentence thesis, Triple P, 12–15% overweight trim to 6–8%, 3-trigger weekly exit).
- **Key rules/thresholds:** 15–25 stocks max (BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12); trim >12%–15% weights back to 6%–8% (BS-Lec12); hard exit on promoter pledge/auditor exit/negative OCF + rising debt (BS-Lec12); 4–8 quarter time stop-loss (BS-Lec10, BS-Lec12).
- **Read more:** BS-Lec04 (Stage 4), BS-Lec10 (Stage 10), BS-Lec11 (Stage 10), BS-Lec12 (Stages 2–7).

## 5. MASTER FRAMEWORK / CHECKLIST
- **Assess Market Regime & Index Breadth:** Check Nifty 50 P/E (< 20x lump-sum; 19x–20x deployment), Chartink % > 200 EMA (12%–15% panic SIP zone), and % > 20 EMA (< 15% + Forward P/E < 20x) to size overall equity exposure and match entry patterns (bull breakout vs. sideways pullback vs. bear reversal) (BS-Lec08, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12).
- **Identify Strong Sectors (Top-Down 67% Group Move):** Run 10-week/3–6 month RRG (Improving → Leading), bracketed equi-weighted custom indices vs. Nifty 500, ratio charts reclaiming 30W EMA, and Tijori/Stock Scans (≥2 peers turning Monthly V-Stop positive) (BS-Lec08).
- **Apply Munger's Inversion ("When NOT to Buy"):** Immediately reject primary Stage 4 downtrends, earnings downgrade cycles, peak margins/peak order books, QIP/100x–300x P/E froth, governance red flags (pledging, auditor/CFO exits, OCF < PAT + rising debt), and Weekly RSI > 83–85 (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec07, BS-Lec08, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec12).
- **Select Archetype & Verify Fundamental Inflection:** Classify the bet—GARP (PEG < 1.5x + NOS triggers/≥+5% EPS revisions), Momentum ("Purple Patch"/Young Momentum), Pre-Opex (CWIP > 50% at 30–40% utilization), Convexity/Mix Shift, Special Situation (clean demerger/self-help, ~8x EV/EBITDA M&A), Bombed-Out IPO (15–24 mo base), Speed Breaker, Pessimism (4–8 qtr downcycle), or Value (P/B < 1x, PEG < 1x, >6%–7% yield) (BS-Lec07, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11).
- **Confirm Technical Entry & Volume Footprint:** Verify >₹400–500 Cr mcap, Stage 2 / Early Stage 2 breakout out of an 8–12 week (or multi-year) base, Volume > 20-MA + upward OBV slope, CRS > benchmark, and green V-Stop + ADX > 20 (or SOIC LTI with ADX = 0; 30W EMA pointing up + Weekly V-Stop for satellites) (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec02, BS-Lec03, BS-Lec05, BS-Lec06, BS-Lec07, BS-Lec09).
- **Size Position, Write 4-Sentence Thesis & Pyramid:** Cap portfolio at 15–25 stocks; enter in 2–4 tranches max (or 2%–4%–6%–8% for uncertain speed breakers), document a 4-sentence buy thesis with exit triggers, and pyramid only on upward-sloping Parabolic SAR (0.04/0.05) or CRS/20W EMA pullbacks while L3 valuation holds (BS-Lec04, BS-Lec09, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12).
- **Execute Objective Exits & Redeploy:** Trim >12%–15% overweight winners to 6%–8%; exit immediately on hard governance breaks, Triple P + Reverse DCF extremes, 4–8 quarter time stop-losses, or weekly 30W EMA + 26W RS + V-Stop (10, 2) triggers (10W/21W EMA for hyper-growth/recent IPOs; tighten to 10W EMA if >70% above 30W EMA) (BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12).

## 8. COMMON MISTAKES & RED FLAGS ACROSS THE MODULE
- **Price-anchoring & break-even anchoring:** Refusing to buy Stage 2 stocks because they rose 5%–10% or holding losers to "get back to cost" instead of anchoring to forward earnings and risk-reward (BS-Lec07, BS-Lec08, BS-Lec12).
- **Averaging down into Stage 4 or broken fundamentals:** Adding to impaired stories (Yes Bank, DHFL, Gensol) or ignoring negative OCF + rising debt, promoter pledging, and CFO/auditor exits (BS-Lec01, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec12).
- **Mismatched time horizons & "forced long-term investing":** Mixing momentum multiples with fundamental hope, holding 100x+ P/E or QIP-frothy stocks into Stage 4, or calling a broken trade a "long-term investment" (BS-Lec07, BS-Lec08, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec11).
- **Using the wrong indicator for exits:** Selling via lagging ADX < 20, standalone Parabolic SAR, standalone CRS, downward OBV, or overbought RSI (>70–90) instead of V-Stop + 30W EMA + RS (BS-Lec02, BS-Lec03, BS-Lec04, BS-Lec06, BS-Lec12).
- **Buying cheap valuations without catalysts or time stops:** Entering Stage 4 value/sector traps (PSU Banks 2014–21, Karnataka Bank, print media yields) before ratio charts/technicals turn or without a 4–8 quarter time stop-loss (BS-Lec08, BS-Lec10, BS-Lec12).
- **Over-tranching & portfolio bloat:** Splitting positions into 10–12 tranches (inflating cost in downtrends), holding 80–90 stocks, or reacting to daily RRG / "Monsoon Frog" noise (BS-Lec04, BS-Lec08, BS-Lec11, BS-Lec12).
