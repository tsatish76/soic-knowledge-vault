## BS-Lec01: Holy Grail of Buying & Selling (SOIC Way): Stage Analysis, Volume, V-Stop, RSI, and Comparative Relative Strength
Duration: 02:06:14 | TVGP focus: Valuation, Growth, Theme Detailed report: BS_Lec01_Report.md
### 1. Core thesis
Valuation-based exits are inherently subjective, and most companies experience temporary 3–6 year "purple patches" rather than permanent 45% CAGR compounding. Combining fundamental growth triggers with Weinstein Stage Analysis, volume ("weight of the evidence"), Monthly Volatility Stop (V-Stop), Weekly RSI, and Comparative Relative Strength (CRS) enforces objective exit discipline and prevents Stage 4 capital destruction.
### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **Weinstein Stage Analysis (Stages 1–4)**: Ride Stage 1 (Basing) and Stage 2 (Markup) while systematically exiting before Stage 4 (Breakdown) to avoid -50% drawdowns that require +100% recovery. → BS-Lec01 – Stages 5 & 10
- **Chart setup & Volume confirmation**: Use Monthly (core exits) and Weekly (satellite/entries) charts with LOG scale and Auto ON; treat expanding volume in >₹400–500 Cr stocks as institutional conviction. → BS-Lec01 – Stages 6–7
- **Volatility Stop (V-Stop) exit system**: V-Stop = ATR(14) × Multiplier on Monthly charts (Length = 10); hold while green sits below candles and exit when red + appears above candles. → BS-Lec01 – Stage 8
- **Weekly RSI + CRS entry/confirmation filter**: Block fresh buys when Weekly RSI > 83–85, track RSI 50 support, and confirm index outperformance via CRS vs. Nifty 500 / Bank Nifty. → BS-Lec01 – Stage 9
### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas

| **Rule/Formula** | **Value/Condition** | **Stage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EPS revision impact | >5% upward / ≥5% downward PAT revision shifts performance | Stage 3 |
| Analyst accuracy | >60% estimates wrong; use facts, ignore target prices | Stage 3 |
| Institutional mcap floor | >₹400–500 Cr (institutions drive volume moves) | Stage 7 |
| Monthly V-Stop settings | Length = 10; Mult 2.5 (core), 3 (high conviction), 1.5–2 (cyclical / 100x P/E) | Stage 8 |
| Weekly RSI thresholds | >83–85 avoid fresh buy; 50 compounder support; <30 deep pessimism | Stage 9 |
| Asymmetric loss math | -50% loss requires +100% gain to break even | Stage 10 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Alkyl Amines & Gland Pharma [?]** → Alkyl re-rated 12–13x→70x P/E (38%→18–20% margin reversion); Gland fell ₹3,694→₹1,450 while analysts cut targets from ₹4,550→₹3,700 → Stage 3 [0:10:21–0:21:46]
- **Eicher Motors, Tata Elxsi & Relaxo** → Monthly V-Stop rode Eicher ₹39→₹3,300 (exit ₹2,500–2,700), Relaxo ₹250→₹1,100 (1st exit in 6 yrs), and Tata Elxsi ₹900→₹7,200–8,700 → Stage 8 [1:32:25–1:38:38]
- **Yes Bank & DHFL vs. South Indian Bank & Equitas** → Averaging down into Stage 4 destroyed capital; South Indian Bank (3x P/E + volume blast) and Equitas (RSI <30 at ₹42–43) paired value with technical confirmation → Stages 5, 7, 9 [0:50:13–0:52:39, 1:20:08–1:23:21, 1:51:09–1:55:51]
### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Relying on sell-side target prices or averaging down into Stage 4 breakdowns [0:19:32–0:21:46, 0:50:13–0:52:39].
- Chasing fresh buys/SIPs when Weekly RSI > 83–85 [1:42:07–1:43:49].
- Holding 100x P/E stocks without tightening V-Stop to 1.5–2x [1:35:12–1:36:38].
- Confusing temporary black-swan/seasonal dips (COVID, VBL Q3) with structural exits [0:35:41–0:37:32].
### 6. Decisive takeaway
Define your investor framework before entering any stock, then pair fundamental growth catalysts with Monthly V-Stop (Length = 10, 2.5x core / 1.5–2x cyclical or 100x P/E) and TradingView alerts. Avoid fresh buys above Weekly RSI 83–85 and never average down into Stage 4 breakdowns.

## BS-Lec02: Mastering ADX (Average Directional Index) + Volatility Stop for Trend Breakouts and Cyclical Timing
Duration: 00:30:13 | TVGP focus: Growth, Valuation Detailed report: BS_Lec02_Report.md
### 1. Core thesis
Combining J. Welles Wilder's **ADX (Average Directional Index)** with **Volatility Stop (V-Stop)** lets investors avoid multi-year sideways consolidations and time Stage 2 breakouts. ADX (>20/25) is a strong buy filter when paired with green V-Stop, but lags on exits—so V-Stop (2.5 Monthly for compounders, 1.5–2.0 Weekly for cyclicals) must execute exits.
### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **ADX + DI setup**: Plot ADX and DI on free TradingView (blue = ADX, green = +DI, red = -DI, level = 20 or 25) to read trend strength (0–100) and directional control. → BS-Lec02 – Stages 1–2
- **Mandatory ADX + V-Stop pairing**: Enter only when **BOTH** ADX > 20/25 (+DI > -DI) **AND** V-Stop turns green; keep allocation small during long ADX < 25 bases. → BS-Lec02 – Stages 3–4
- **Headwind & cyclical exit protocol**: Exit on V-Stop (1.5–2.0 Weekly for 6–9 mo cyclicals; 2.5 Monthly for compounders) whenever -DI makes higher highs and V-Stop turns red, rather than waiting for lagging ADX < 20. → BS-Lec02 – Stages 4–5
### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas

| **Rule/Formula** | **Value/Condition** | **Stage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ADX strength zones | 0–25 weak; >20 emerging; 25–50 strong; 50–75 very strong; 75–100 super trend | Stage 2 |
| Combined buy trigger | ADX > 20/25 (25 fewer whipsaws) + +DI > -DI + V-Stop Green | Stages 3–4 |
| Cyclical Weekly V-Stop | Weekly chart, Multiplier = 1.5–2.0 (6–9 mo cycles) | Stages 3, 5 |
| Practice benchmark | Backtest on 300–400 to 500–1,000 charts | Stages 4–5 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Bhansali Engineering Polymers** → Avoided 2011–16 base; ADX >20 at ₹27 → ADX 86, ₹187 (7–8 bagger); ADX <20 exit at ₹60 proved V-Stop 1.5–2.0 is superior for selling → Stage 3 [00:08:25–00:13:20]
- **Navin Fluorine & Syngene** → Navin ADX >25 at ₹118→₹802 & re-entry ₹1,372; Syngene 22-mo base (ADX <25) warrants low allocation until breakout → Stage 3 [00:13:20–00:16:55]
- **Aarti Industries & Gujarat Ambuja Exports** → Aarti exited at ₹767–847 on V-Stop while -DI rose and ADX lagged; Gujarat Ambuja exited ~₹300 on Weekly V-Stop → Stage 4 [00:18:07–00:21:23]
- **HEG, Deepak Fertilizers & Surya Roshni** → Weekly V-Stop 2.0 captured HEG (₹200→₹3,600–3,700, ADX 91.5) and Deepak Fert (₹220→₹711); Surya Roshni (ADX <20) shows waiting discipline → Stage 5 [00:22:22–00:27:43]
### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Using ADX < 20 to sell instead of V-Stop, sacrificing 50%+ of multi-bagger gains [00:12:45–00:13:20].
- Buying on ADX alone without green V-Stop, or jumping in before ADX crosses 20 (Surya Roshni) [00:21:41–00:22:22, 00:25:52].
- Tying up full capital in flat ADX < 20/25 consolidations [00:16:32, 00:27:43].
### 6. Decisive takeaway
Use ADX > 20/25 paired with +DI > -DI and green V-Stop to catch Stage 2 breakouts out of long consolidations, and always let V-Stop (2.5 Monthly for compounders, 1.5–2.0 Weekly for cyclicals) trigger your exit before lagging ADX rolls over.

## BS-Lec03: How Relative Strength Combined with VStop Is Used to Buy Stocks
Duration: 00:16:44 | TVGP focus: Not TVGP-specific (Valuation referenced via Reverse DCF in Stage 3) Detailed report: BS_Lec03_Report.md
### 1. Core thesis
Relative Strength (RS) identifies stocks outperforming a benchmark index or sector peers, making it useful for both momentum and value investing. Pairing Comparative Relative Strength (CRS) with Volatility Stop (V-Stop) and high volume filters out multi-year dead money and confirms exact entry and pyramiding-up points.
### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **3-Factor Buy Confluence:** Align CRS diverging upward above the benchmark line, green/upward V-Stop, and high trading volume to time entries → BS-Lec03 – Stage 2 [00:05:10–00:06:30].
- **Automated Mean-Reversion Alerts:** Set TradingView crossing alerts on CRS vs. benchmark (pop-up or email) after identifying fundamentally improving value stocks → BS-Lec03 – Stage 2 [00:07:05–00:08:11].
- **Compounder Pyramiding:** Hold when V-Stop stays intact during RS consolidation, and pyramid up when CRS crosses back above Nifty 50 → BS-Lec03 – Stage 3 [00:08:39–00:09:58].
- **StockEdge → TradingView Funnel:** Run StockEdge RS Price Scans, export to Excel, upload as a TradingView watchlist, and verify Weekly/Monthly V-Stop + CRS → BS-Lec03 – Stage 4 [00:10:52–00:14:40].
- **Peer-to-Peer CRS:** Compare two peer stocks directly on Weekly/Monthly charts to spot sector leadership rotation → BS-Lec03 – Stage 5 [00:14:40–00:15:16].
### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas

| **Rule/Formula** | **Value/Condition** | **Stage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRS Outperformance | Gray line > Blue benchmark line (trending up) | Stage 2 |
| Asymmetric Roles | RS = Buy & Pyramid; V-Stop = Sell/Exit | Stage 2, 3 |

## BS-Lec04: When to Hold, Buy, Pyramid & Sell using Technicals for Long Term (VSTOP, ADX, Parabolic SAR, RSI, Comparative Strength & OBV)
Duration: 00:22:08 | TVGP focus: Not TVGP-specific Detailed report: BS_Lec04_Report.md
### 1. Core thesis
Long-term investors need objective rules to initiate core compounder positions, average up without inflating cost basis, and avoid premature shakeouts during consolidations. Combining trend-strength (ADX > 20) and volatility trailing stops (VSTOP) with upward-sloping Parabolic SAR for pyramiding—and confirming monthly entries with weekly RSI, Comparative Strength, and OBV—turns discretionary chart reading into a repeatable, probability-based system.
### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **Monthly Buy & Pyramid Checklist:** Initiate buys when VSTOP is positive (green) and ADX > 20 (with DI+/DI- hidden); pyramid only when VSTOP is green, ADX > 20, and Parabolic SAR (0.04/0.05 increment) dots sit below candles and point upwards → BS-Lec04 – Stage 3.
- **Tranche Allocation & VSTOP Exit Framework:** Build target 3%–6% allocations in 2–4 tranches max so average cost stays below CMP, and exit solely when VSTOP turns red (2.0x multiplier for cyclicals; 2.5x for compounders) → BS-Lec04 – Stage 4.
- **Weekly Probability Boosters (Multi-Timeframe Confluence):** Validate monthly entry zones on weekly charts by requiring RSI > 50 with high volume, Comparative Relative Strength outperforming Nifty 50 or Nifty Bank, and rising On Balance Volume (OBV) → BS-Lec04 – Stage 6.
### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas

| **Rule / Formula** | **Value / Condition** | **Stage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial Buy (Monthly) | VSTOP positive (green) + ADX > 20 | Stage 3, 5 |
| Pyramiding Trigger | VSTOP green + ADX > 20 + Parabolic SAR (0.04/0.05) pointing upwards | Stage 3, 5 |
| Tranche Limit | 2–4 tranches max for 3%–6% allocation (never 10–12) | Stage 4 |
| VSTOP Exit (Cyclicals) | Multiplier = 2.0x (turns red) | Stage 4, 5 |
| VSTOP Exit (Compounders) | Multiplier = 2.5x (turns red) | Stage 4, 5 |
| Weekly RSI Confirmation | Crosses > 50 with high volume (50 = support in uptrend) | Stage 6 |
| Comparative Strength | Outperforming Nifty 50 or Nifty Bank | Stage 6 |
| On Balance Volume (OBV) | Rising (plus-volume days > negative-volume days) | Stage 6 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Divi's Laboratories** → Monthly DI+/DI- off setup triggered buy at ADX > 20 (~35) + positive VSTOP + high volume, with upward-sloping SAR pyramiding zones → Stage 3 [00:05:48–00:07:54].
- **Dixon Technologies** → 3% allocation built in 3 tranches (₹1,100–₹1,700, avg ~₹1,400) and held via VSTOP until ₹3,800 exit (~1.5x–1.7x [? verify]) → Stage 4 [00:07:54–00:09:46].
- **Laurus Labs** → Max 4 tranches (₹183 entry, 4th tranche ₹314, avg ₹220–₹250) exited cleanly on VSTOP at ₹500–₹520 → Stage 4 [00:09:46–00:12:06].
- **PI Industries** → 2.5x VSTOP multiplier held from 2013 to March 2020 with only one trigger in 12–13 years, avoiding SAR's ₹689–₹700 shakeout → Stage 4 [00:12:06–00:13:36].
- **Equitas Small Finance Bank** → Weekly early entry (5th Dec at ₹56) vs. monthly (₹51–₹57) with 2.0x cyclical VSTOP and weekly 4-signal confluence vs. Nifty Bank (RSI 72.1) → Stage 4, 6 [00:14:06–00:15:17, 00:18:43–00:19:33].
### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Never use Parabolic SAR standalone for buying or selling; it cannot measure trend strength and causes whipsaws and portfolio churn [00:02:40–00:03:35].
- Never pyramid during sideways Parabolic SAR consolidation or when VSTOP is negative [00:07:26–00:07:54, 00:15:47–00:16:16].
- Never split a position into 10–12 tranches; fundamental downtrends will inflate average buying cost [00:10:16–00:11:30].
- Backtest hybrid timeframe systems (e.g., weekly buy, monthly sell) and never switch rules mid-trade [00:14:06–00:14:52].
### 6. Decisive takeaway
Enter core holdings only when VSTOP is positive and ADX > 20, pyramid in 2–4 tranches using upward-sloping Parabolic SAR (0.04/0.05), and exit strictly on VSTOP (2.0x cyclicals, 2.5x compounders). Validate monthly setups on weekly charts with RSI > 50, benchmark Comparative Strength, and rising OBV to stack probabilistic conviction.

## BS-Lec05: Moving Averages (SMA vs. EMA), 30-Weekly EMA + VSTOP & Golden Crossover/Crossdown for Satellite Portfolios
Duration: 00:33:52 | TVGP focus: Not TVGP-specific Detailed report: BS_Lec05_Report.md
### 1. Core thesis
Exponential Moving Averages (EMA) weight recent prices more heavily than Simple Moving Averages (SMA), triggering timelier exits when overvalued stocks reverse. Combining the 30-weekly EMA with weekly VSTOP (2x, length 10) gives satellite and cyclical portfolios disciplined dual-confirmation entries and early Stage 4 exits.
### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **Weekly EMA Setup:** Apply EMA 10 (or 13), EMA 30, and EMA 40 max on weekly charts ("Same as chart") to spot base formation, uptrends, downtrends, and Stage 1→2 transitions → BS-Lec05 – Stage 2, 3 [00:06:36–00:14:49].
- **30W EMA + Weekly VSTOP (Satellite PF):** Enter only when both 30W EMA points up and weekly VSTOP (2x, 10) is green; exit early when weekly VSTOP turns red → BS-Lec05 – Stage 4, 6 [00:20:36–00:24:43, 00:32:59–00:33:33].
- **Golden Crossover & Cross Down:** 10W EMA crossing above 30W EMA signals bullish momentum (best for stock screening); crossing below signals bearishness (satellite cyclical exits) → BS-Lec05 – Stage 5 [00:25:31–00:30:01].
- **Core vs. Satellite Split:** Manage high-ROCE/high-cash-flow core holdings with monthly VSTOP, and riskier cyclical satellite holdings with weekly MAs + VSTOP → BS-Lec05 – Stage 3, 6 [00:18:10–00:18:41, 00:33:52].
### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas

| **Rule/Formula** | **Value/Condition** | **Stage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly-to-Daily Map | 10W = 50D; 30W = 150D; 40W = 200D (× 5 trading days) | Stage 1 |
| SMA vs. EMA | Prefer EMA (recent price weight) over SMA (equal weight) | Stage 2 |
| 30W EMA Leeway | Allow 2–3 weeks support check; exit when 30W EMA slopes down | Stage 3 |
| Satellite Dual Entry | 30W EMA pointing UP + Weekly VSTOP (2x, 10) GREEN | Stage 4, 6 |
| Strict No-Buy Filter | Never enter if VSTOP is green while 30W EMA points down | Stage 4 |
| Golden Crossover / Down | 10W EMA crossing above / below 30W EMA on weekly chart | Stage 5 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Equitas SFB** → Weekly EMA 10/30 Stage 2 breakout on high volume; 10W/30W Golden Crossover demo → Stage 3, 5 [00:11:42–00:14:49, 00:25:43–00:27:15].
- **Bhansali Engg [?] & Rain Industries** → 30W EMA captured trends (₹24→₹165; ₹35→₹332) but whipsawed in choppy phases → Stage 3 [00:14:49–00:18:10].
- **Heranba [?] & Aarti Drugs** → Avoid post-IPO falling swords (downward 30W EMA); enter after 2019–2020 sideways base + high-volume Stage 2 breakout → Stage 3 [00:18:41–00:20:36].
- **Sequent [?], Natural Capsules, HEG & Laurus Labs** → Weekly VSTOP exited early (₹462, ₹3,681–₹3,700, ₹644) ahead of 30W EMA lag → Stage 4 [00:21:07–00:25:31].
- **Divi's Labs, Saregama & Alkyl Amines** → 10W/30W crossover held Divi's ~4 yrs (₹728→₹4,800); exited Saregama (₹438) & Alkyl Amines (₹3,400) → Stage 5 [00:27:48–00:31:04].
### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Never apply this EMA framework on monthly charts—use weekly charts [00:12:18–00:12:50].
- Do not rely on 30W EMA in sideways markets (whipsaws); use it there only to spot base formation [00:14:49–00:17:14].
- Never catch post-IPO falling swords whose 30W EMA points continuously down [00:18:41–00:19:32].
- Never buy on green VSTOP alone while 30W EMA points down; expect higher churn on weekly crossovers [00:21:57, 00:24:09–00:24:43, 00:29:38].
### 6. Decisive takeaway
For satellite and cyclical holdings, pair the 30-weekly EMA with weekly VSTOP (2x, length 10) so you only enter when both align upward and exit early when VSTOP turns red. Use 10W/30W Golden Crossovers primarily to screen stocks and manage cyclical exits while keeping core compounders on monthly VSTOP.

## BS-Lec06: Volumes — Power of Volumes Study: 20-MA of Volume and On-Balance Volume (OBV)
Duration: 00:16:35 | TVGP focus: Promoter (insider/promoter buying triangulation) Detailed report: BS_Lec06_Report.md
### 1. Core thesis
Price reveals what is happening in a stock, while volume provides the "weight of the evidence" confirming whether institutions and smart money are backing the move [00:01:40–00:02:11, 00:16:01]. Volume behavior is asymmetric across market stages: launching a Stage 2 breakout requires surging "engine power," whereas a brutal Stage 4 decline can begin even on very low volume [00:00:49–00:01:40, 00:14:55]. Combining raw volume, the 20-period Volume Moving Average, and On-Balance Volume (OBV) slope sharpens Stage 2 breakout probability alongside V-Stop, ADX > 20, and Parabolic SAR [00:02:11–00:02:43, 00:12:17].
### 2. Frameworks & methods
- **Volume Asymmetry (Stage 1–4 Cycle):** Stage 2 breakouts require high, expanding volume in the breakout direction; Stage 4 declines need only a push and can start on low volume → BS-Lec06 – Stage 1 [00:00:00–00:02:11].
- **20-Period Volume Moving Average (MA Length = 20):** Plot the 20-MA over TradingView volume bars; bars rising substantially above 20-MA alongside green V-Stop and promoter buying signal smart-money accumulation → BS-Lec06 – Stage 3 [00:05:05–00:07:25].
- **Lifetime-High Volume Check:** Pause weekly/monthly breakout charts to verify if current volume bars are the highest ever in the stock's history, catching smart money before lagging indicators confirm → BS-Lec06 – Stage 3 [00:08:29–00:09:03].
- **On-Balance Volume (OBV) Slope:** Cumulative volume indicator adding volume on up-closes and subtracting on down-closes; track strictly the **slope** (upward = accumulation, downward = distribution, flat = neutral) → BS-Lec06 – Stage 4 [00:09:03–00:12:17].
- **Stage 2 Breakout Volume Screen:** Screen fundamentally strong stocks consolidating and breaking out with Volume > 20-MA and upward-sloping OBV → BS-Lec06 – Stage 5 [00:15:29–00:16:01].
### 3. Rules, thresholds & formulas

| **Rule / Formula** | **Value / Condition** | **Stage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume 20-MA Setup | TradingView Volume indicator, MA Length = 20 | Stage 3 |
| Smart-Money Surge | Volume bars substantially above 20-MA + rising price | Stage 3 |
| OBV Up-Close / Down-Close | Close > Prior Close: + Vol; Close < Prior Close: - Vol | Stage 4 |
| OBV Interpretation | Upward slope = accumulation; Downward = distribution; Flat = neutral | Stage 4 |
| Stage 2 Breakout Screen | Consolidation breakout + Volume > 20-MA + upward OBV slope | Stage 5 |
| Exit Priority | V-Stop triggers exits faster than OBV during slow distribution | Stage 4 |

### 4. Key examples
- **Varun Beverages** → MSCI index inclusion (30 Nov) triggered a massive volume bar and rapid move from ₹1,249 to ₹1,410 → Stage 2 [00:02:43–00:03:48].
- **Linc Pen [?]** → Above-20-MA volume + green V-Stop aligned with ~₹1.62 Cr (₹2–3 Cr [?]) promoter open-market buys in a ₹300–400 Cr mcap → Stage 3 [00:05:05–00:07:25].
- **Equitas** → Golden Crossover with rising volume bars, lifetime-high weekly volume check (13–20 Feb 2023 [? verify]), and OBV slope transitions → Stage 2–4 [00:03:48, 00:08:00–00:12:17].
- **Laurus Labs** → Broke out of ₹106–₹108 base to ₹707 on rising volume; Warburg Pincus ₹66 sale (4 May) drove OBV down before Stage 2 accumulation; V-Stop exited before OBV turned down → Stage 2, Stage 4 [00:03:48–00:04:30, 00:12:44–00:13:45].
- **Balaji Amines [?]** → Post-2021 volume flattening below 20-MA signaled waning buying interest → Stage 5 [00:14:20–00:15:29].
### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Never dismiss a Stage 4 breakdown because volume is low—stocks need heavy volume to climb, but only a push to fall → Stage 1, Stage 5 [00:01:15–00:01:40, 00:14:55].
- Ignore absolute OBV values and flat/stagnant OBV; focus strictly on directional slope → Stage 4 [00:10:42–00:12:17].
- Do not wait for OBV to slope downward to exit; slow distribution means V-Stop exits much sooner → Stage 4 [00:13:14].
### 6. Decisive takeaway

- **Exit Chakravyuh & Pre-Entry Checklist:** Define horizon (Swing, Positional, Medium-term, Compounder) and write a 4-sentence buy thesis with objective exit triggers before entering to eliminate break-even anchoring → BS-Lec12 – Stage 2, Stage 3 [0:42:08–0:44:55, 0:59:00–1:01:06].
- **Three Lethal Ps (Triple P) & Reverse DCF:** Exit when Peak Margins, Peak Profitability, and Peak Sentiment (Forbes covers, "Eiffel Tower" surges) meet growth deceleration and Bull/Base/Bear valuation compression → BS-Lec12 – Stage 3, Stage 4 [1:02:10–1:13:38, 1:23:19–1:25:40].
- **3-Trigger Technical Exit System:** Execute emotionless weekly exits when 30W EMA turns negative, 26W Relative Strength vs. Nifty 500 turns red, and V-Stop (10, 2) triggers (accepting 10%–25% giveback) → BS-Lec12 – Stage 5 [1:41:36–1:55:30].
- **Recent IPO & Parabolic Climax Tightening:** Use 10W/21W EMA + RS for IPOs lacking 30W history, and tighten stops to 10W EMA when price stretches > 70% above 30W EMA → BS-Lec12 – Stage 6 [1:58:51–2:03:01].

### 5. Cautions & red flags
- Never average down into impaired businesses or wait for break-even prices; ego is the investor's worst enemy.
- Never sell on overbought RSI (> 70–90) or reverse-rationalize a fundamental story after deciding to sell on P&L.
- Flag PPND stocks ("Permanently Pregnant, Never Delivers") where con-call optimism contradicts 5-year flat sales/profits.
- Avoid holding 80–90 stocks or concentrating heavily where regulatory shocks (F&O rules, market coupling, GST) can strike.
### 6. Decisive takeaway
Define your exit triggers in writing before buying, and enforce an objective combination of hard governance exits, 4–8 quarter time stop-losses, and weekly 30W EMA + RS + V-Stop breaks. Systematic exits protect compounding by preserving capital in Stage 4 downtrends so it can be redeployed at 19x–20x Nifty P/E or panic breadth zones.
