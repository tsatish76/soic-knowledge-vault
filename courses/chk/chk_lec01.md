## Part A: 3-Minute Summary Reference Card (D2)

* **Core Thesis:** Long-term multi-baggers in India occur at the intersection of a non-linear theme, reasonable entry valuation, visible capacity growth, and honest capital allocators. Skipping any single pillar leads to capital impairment.
* **Master Rules & Benchmarks Table:**

| Metric / Parameter | SOIC Benchmark | Cautions & Red Flags |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme Growth** | Industry growth > 12–15% CAGR | Avoid sectors dependent solely on government tariffs |
| **Valuation PEG** | PEG < 2.0x for compounders | High P/E with margin contraction causes double-derating |
| **ROCE** | 5-Year Avg ROCE > 18% | Watch out for accounting ROCE inflated by low asset base |
| **Promoter Pledge** | Strictly 0% (or < 5% with reduction roadmap) | Any sudden increase in pledge warrants immediate review |

## Part B: Detailed Lecture Report (D1)

### [00:00] Stage 1: The Philosophy of TVGP
The speaker opens by contrasting classical Western value investing (cigar butts) with the Indian economic reality: high-cost of capital, regulatory shifts, and emerging consumption habits. The TVGP framework acts as a filtration sieve.

### [24:15] Stage 2: Theme & Megatrends (T)
Theme analysis is not macro forecasting; it is identifying structural shifts. Examples cited include formalization in building materials and chemical supply chain shifts away from China.

### [58:40] Stage 3: Valuation Discipline (V)
Never buy a business solely because it is a "good company". Even compounders generate 0% 5-year returns if bought at peak bull market multiples.

### [01:34:10] Stage 4: Promoter Forensics & Integrity (P)
Promoter integrity is evaluated through:
1. Auditor remuneration as a percentage of revenue.
2. Frequency of CFO or auditor resignations.
3. Related-party transactions with promoter-owned unlisted entities.

## Self-Test Q&A

::: details Q1: Why is high ROCE alone insufficient if the Theme is negative?
Even if a company earns 30% ROCE, if the overall addressable market shrinks (e.g. print media), the business cannot reinvest its profits at that rate. Cash piles up or is squandered on bad diversifications.
:::

::: details Q2: What is the maximum acceptable promoter pledge?
SOIC benchmark is strictly 0%. Any pledge above 10% is an automatic disqualifier for core portfolio allocations.
:::
