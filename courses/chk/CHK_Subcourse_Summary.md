### 1. OVERARCHING THEME & OBJECTIVE

Active equity outperformance in Indian stock markets requires transitioning from static single-stock bottom-up analysis to systematic top-down thematic discovery, quantitative growth screening, forensic promoter evaluation, and scenario-grounded valuation modeling (SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec03, TVGP-Lec08, SOIC-Lec13). The sub-course equips investors with the decision capability to identify structural macro tailwinds, capture non-linear earnings surprises at the "Positive Surprise" stage of expectation life cycles, audit promoter capital allocation over a 10-year retained earnings horizon, and execute forward P&L scenario models (Bull, Base, Bear) while enforcing strict technical and time stop-losses to protect capital across market cycles (SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05, SOIC-Lec06, Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec12).

The curriculum aligns directly with the TVGP Analysis Architecture (Theme, Valuation, Growth, Promoter):
*   **Theme**: Serves as the primary prerequisite pillar, explaining 60% of stock return attribution (SOIC-Lec01, SOIC-Lec06). It covers global top-down dot-connecting, supply-side capital cycles, government policy discovery (PLI, DAP 2020), and sub-sector custom relative strength (RS) index screening (SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec03).
*   **Growth**: Evaluates Profit After Tax (PAT) growth compounding, the "EXPANDING" growth checklist, operational CATALYST triggers, debottlenecking CapEx hierarchies, and J-curve rate-of-change (RoC) earnings inflections (SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05, SOIC-Lec06, Course-Lec07).
*   **Valuation**: Applied primarily as a tool of avoidance to eliminate value and growth traps (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10). It anchors entry multiples (15x–35x P/E) to expected PAT growth %, calculates double-engine compounding (P/E × EPS), evaluates 200-day moving average (DMA) market sentiment breadth, and applies sector-specific valuation frameworks (Banking ROA tree, deep-cyclical P/B anchor, auto ancillary kit-value expansion) (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11, TVGP-Lec12, SOIC-Lec13).
*   **Promoter**: Audits governance via the 10-year retained capital deployment formula, a Red/Yellow/Green Traffic Light capital allocation framework, 6 softer warning indicators, and credit rating agency forensic audits (SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec08, TVGP-Lec12).

### 3. CORE PILLARS OF THE MODULE

#### Pillar 1: Top-Down Thematic & Capital Cycle Primacy
*   **Why it matters**: Catching an active top-down industry tailwind generates automatic sales growth regardless of company-specific nuances, whereas holding a high-quality company in a stagnant sector guarantees years of capital stasis or severe P/E derating (TVGP-Lec03, SOIC-Lec09). Empirical backtesting demonstrates that macro themes and sector trends account for 80% of total equity return attribution (60% theme, 20% sector, 20% stock selection) (SOIC-Lec06).
*   **Development across lectures**: Introduced in SOIC-Lec01 via the Marathon Capital Cycle Model (tracking supply additions across 4 stages) and Druckenmiller 3-Pillar Timing → Applied in TVGP-Lec02 & TVGP-Lec03 through global top-down dot-connecting, the 5-step selection funnel, custom Stage 2 RS sector indices, and the sector financial divergence rule → Refined in SOIC-Lec06 & Course-Lec07 via Claude AI return attribution backtesting, TGPV evaluation hierarchy, and the CATALYST theme trigger framework.
*   **Key rules & thresholds**:
    *   Return attribution: 80% driven by Theme + Sector (SOIC-Lec06).
    *   O'Neil Winner Repeat Rule: Only 25% of past bull market leaders repeat in subsequent cycles (SOIC-Lec01).
    *   Capital cycle duration: Industry boom-to-bust cycles span 5 to 8 years (SOIC-Lec01).
    *   Sector Financial Divergence Rule: 4 of 5 peers profitable = valid sector tailwind; 5 of 5 loss-making = broken theme (TVGP-Lec03).
*   **Read more**: SOIC-Lec01 (Report Stages 1, 3, 6, 9), TVGP-Lec02 (Report Stages 1, 9), TVGP-Lec03 (Report Stages 1, 3, 12), SOIC-Lec06 (Report Stages 7, 8), Course-Lec07 (Report Stage 1).

#### Pillar 2: Technical Stage Analysis & Relative Strength (RS) Execution
*   **Why it matters**: Coupling fundamental thesis discovery with quantitative price-volume action prevents catching falling knives during Stage 4 downtrends and avoids capital tie-ups in Stage 1 bases (TVGP-Lec02). Systematic chart execution provides objective entry triggers during early Stage 2 breakouts and disciplined exit rules when technical structures deteriorate (TVGP-Lec02, Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec08).
*   **Development across lectures**: Introduced in TVGP-Lec02 using Stan Weinstein's 4-Stage Analysis (30-week EMA slope), Weekly Vstop signals, and MarketSmith RS Ratings → Applied in SOIC-Lec04 & TVGP-Lec05 via RS sector quadrant scans, 150 DMA extension filters, and volume rockets → Refined in Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec08, TVGP-Lec10 & TVGP-Lec11 through the StockBee 4% scan, Scan Match intersection screening, 30/40-weekly EMA technical stop-losses, and 200 DMA market breadth sentiment gauges.
*   **Key rules & thresholds**:
    *   Stage 2 Entry Conditions: Price > upward-sloping 30-week EMA + Green Weekly Vstop (TVGP-Lec02).
    *   Relative Strength Leaders: MarketSmith RS Rating > 70–80 (top quartile) (TVGP-Lec02, SOIC-Lec04).
    *   StockBee 4% Rule: Daily price gain \\(\ge 4\%\\), Volume \\(>100\text{k}\\) and \\(>50\\)-day average volume (Course-Lec07).
    *   Parabolic Climax Top Exit: Price extended 70%–80% above 150-day moving average (SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05).
    *   Technical Stop-Loss: Weekly price close below downward-sloping 30-week or 40-week EMA (TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec08).
    *   Market Sentiment Breadth: Extreme panic buy zone at \\(<20\%–25\%\\) stocks above 200 DMA; euphoria exit zone at \\(>85\%–90\%\\) (TVGP-Lec11).
*   **Read more**: TVGP-Lec02 (Report Stages 8, 9, 11, 12), SOIC-Lec04 (Report Stages 1, 3, 13), TVGP-Lec05 (Report Stages 6, 9), Course-Lec07 (Report Stages 3, 8, 9), TVGP-Lec08 (Report Stage 10), TVGP-Lec11 (Report Stage 6).

#### Pillar 3: Rate of Change (Delta) & Non-Linear J-Curve Earnings Acceleration
*   **Why it matters**: Long-term market cap CAGR is overwhelmingly driven by net profit (PAT) compounding (\\(>50\%\\) direct contribution), but non-linear re-rating (J-curve returns) requires positive earnings surprises relative to inbuilt market expectations (SOIC-Lec04, SOIC-Lec06). Identifying operational catalysts before analyst upgrade cycles begin captures massive valuation re-ratings (SOIC-Lec06, Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec11).
*   **Development across lectures**: Introduced in SOIC-Lec04 & TVGP-Lec05 via stock price deconstruction (\\(P = \text{PE} \times \text{EPS}\\)), the EXPANDING growth checklist, deleveraging drivers, and the CapEx ROCE hierarchy → Applied in SOIC-Lec06 & Course-Lec07 using the Surprise Element Model, Three Trains metaphor (Options B/C), 13th-quarter RoC inflections, the CATALYST framework, and the Congrats Index → Refined in SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec11 & SOIC-Lec13 through the rate of change (delta) method, the Double Engine Model, 8–12 quarter analyst upgrade cycles, and sequential P&L waterfall modeling.
*   **Key rules & thresholds**:
    *   Market Cap Fuel: \\(>50\%\\) of long-term market cap CAGR is explained directly by PAT CAGR (SOIC-Lec04).
    *   Fast Growth Filters: YoY PAT growth \\(>20\%–30\%\\) (TVGP-Lec05); YoY top-line revenue growth \\(>15\%\\) across 2–3 quarters (TVGP-Lec05); active PAT growth target \\(\ge 2\text{x}–3\text{x}\\) index growth (minimum 18%+) (Course-Lec07).
    *   Analyst Upgrade Cycle: Fundamental inflections drive analyst EPS upgrade cycles lasting 8 to 12 quarters (2 to 3 years) (SOIC-Lec06).
    *   Market Cap Sweet Spot: Multi-bagger potential concentrates in ₹2,500 to ₹20,000 crore companies, crossing the ₹6,000–₹7,000 crore institutional mutual fund floor (SOIC-Lec01, SOIC-Lec06).
*   **Read more**: SOIC-Lec04 (Report Stages 4, 11, 12), TVGP-Lec05 (Report Stages 2, 3, 7), SOIC-Lec06 (Report Stages 1, 2, 4, 5), Course-Lec07 (Report Stages 1, 9, 10, 11), SOIC-Lec09 (Report Stages 2, 3), TVGP-Lec11 (Report Stages 3, 8), SOIC-Lec13 (Report Stages 1, 4).

#### Pillar 4: Forensic Governance & Promoter Capital Allocation Discipline
*   **Why it matters**: A strong business model can carry a weak manager, but an exceptional manager cannot salvage a flawed business model (TVGP-Lec08). Over a 10-year horizon, management controls over 60% of total corporate capital through retained earnings, making governance checks essential to prevent severe capital loss (TVGP-Lec08).
*   **Development across lectures**: Introduced in SOIC-Lec01 & TVGP-Lec05 via credit rating agency report audits (detecting netted cash flows and recourse discounting) and LLM con-call extraction → Applied in TVGP-Lec08 through the 10-year retained capital deployment formula, the Red/Yellow/Green Traffic Light framework, and 6 softer warning indicators → Refined in TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec12 & SOIC-Lec13 via con-call capital allocation interrogation, promoter restructuring arbitrage, free-float pledging/MTF leverage limits, and statutory provision audits.
*   **Key rules & thresholds**:
    *   10-Year Capital Deployment Formula: \\(1 - \frac{1}{(1+r)^{10}}\\); at \\(r=10\%\\), management controls 61.4% of total capital over 10 years (TVGP-Lec08).
    *   Sandbagged Guidance Rule: Guided growth \\(<\\) Delivered growth (e.g., guided 15% vs. delivered 30%) = Green Light management signal (TVGP-Lec08).
    *   Whistleblower Alert Threshold: \\(>80\%\\) complaint surge in leveraged financial institutions = Red Light warning (TVGP-Lec08).
    *   Asset Sale Dividend Payout: \\(\ge 60\%\\) of asset sale proceeds returned as special dividends = Green Light (TVGP-Lec08).
    *   Capex-to-Market Cap Debt Trap: CapEx \\(\ge\\) Market Cap accompanied by high debt = Severe liquidity crisis risk (TVGP-Lec08).
    *   Promoter Pledging / MTF Limit: Free float pledging or MTF leverage \\(>28\%–29\%\\) triggers automated forced liquidations during drawdowns (TVGP-Lec12).
*   **Read more**: SOIC-Lec01 (Report Stage 10), TVGP-Lec05 (Report Stage 5), TVGP-Lec08 (Report Stages 2, 3, 4, 6, 7), TVGP-Lec10 (Report Stage 12), TVGP-Lec12 (Report Stages 1, 10), SOIC-Lec13 (Report Stage 5).

#### Pillar 5: Valuation as a Tool of Avoidance & Sector-Specific Metric Matching
*   **Why it matters**: Overpaying for growth creates severe growth traps, while buying stagnant businesses without catalysts leads to value traps (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11). Valuation functions as a safety net and avoidance tool, matching starting entry multiples (15x–35x P/E) to expected profit growth and tailoring valuation metrics (P/B, ROA tree, kit-value expansion) to sectoral economic engines (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec12).
*   **Development across lectures**: Introduced in TVGP-Lec02 & TVGP-Lec03 via valuation linkages, Gold lender P/B entry zones (1.0x–1.2x), and capital market P/E ranges (15x–25x) → Applied in SOIC-Lec09 & TVGP-Lec10 through the master valuation equation (\\(\text{Valuation} = \text{Multiples} + \text{Growth Rate}\\)), Growth-to-P/E Parity, the 11-Stage Market Emotion Cycle, the Peak Exit framework, and the equity-to-bond transition at 14x–14.5x P/E → Refined in TVGP-Lec11, TVGP-Lec12 & SOIC-Lec13 via the Double Engine Model, 2-year forward PEG ratios (\\(\le 1.5\text{x}\\)), deep-cyclical P/E inversion and P/B anchors (\\(<1.3\text{x}\\)), banking ROA trees, auto ancillary double engines, and 3-scenario Bull/Base/Bear forward P&L models.
*   **Key rules & thresholds**:
    *   Master Valuation Equation: \\(\text{Valuation} = \text{Multiples} + \text{Growth Rate}\\) (SOIC-Lec09).
    *   Small/Mid-Cap P/E Entry Sweet Spot: 15x–35x P/E where expected PAT Growth \\(\% \ge \text{P/E}\\) (TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11).
    *   High P/E Trap Threshold: \\(>30\text{x}–35\text{x}\\) P/E with flat or decelerating PAT; \\(>50\text{x}\\) P/E has a maximum 12–18 month horizon unless EPS growth \\(>100\%\\) (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10).
    *   PEG Upper Bound: Forward \\(\text{PEG} = \text{P/E} \div \text{EPS Growth } \% \le 1.5\text{x}–1.75\text{x}\\) (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11, SOIC-Lec13).
    *   Implied Bond P/E Benchmark: Equities with flat PAT de-rate to \\(14\text{x}–14.5\text{x}\\) P/E (at a 7% G-Sec interest rate) (SOIC-Lec06, SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10).
    *   Deep-Cyclical Metals Bands: Buy zone at \\(\text{P/B} < 1.0\text{x}–1.3\text{x}\\) near trough margins; peak sell zone at \\(\text{P/B} > 2.5\text{x}\\) or \\(\text{EV/EBITDA} > 6\text{x}\\) (TVGP-Lec12).
    *   Banking ROA/ROE Benchmarks: Target \\(\text{ROA} > 1.0\%–1.15\%\\) and \\(\text{ROE} > 14\%–15\%\\) for P/B re-rating (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec12, SOIC-Lec13).
    *   Research Time Budget: 80%–85% allocated to Theme/Growth (2.5 hours), 15%–20% to Valuation (20–30 minutes) (SOIC-Lec09).
*   **Read more**: TVGP-Lec03 (Report Stages 5, 11), SOIC-Lec09 (Report Stages 1, 7, 8, 10, 11), TVGP-Lec10 (Report Stages 1, 7, 8, 11), TVGP-Lec11 (Report Stages 3, 6, 8, 11), TVGP-Lec12 (Report Stages 4, 7, 8, 11), SOIC-Lec13 (Report Stages 6, 7, 10, 11).

### 5. MASTER FRAMEWORK / CHECKLIST

An investor can execute the following sequential, 8-step actionable process:

1.  **Step 1: Top-Down Thematic & Macro Screening**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Druckenmiller 3-Pillar Timing Model, Marathon Capital Cycle Model, Top-Down Policy & Incentive Discovery (PLI / DAP 2020), Global Dot-Connecting.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Select industries in Stage 1/Stage 4 capital cycles where supply additions have consolidated (SOIC-Lec01); verify policy backing (e.g., PLI \$500B production target, DAP Indian-IDDM preference) (SOIC-Lec01); confirm sector financial divergence (at least 4 of 5 peers profitable) (TVGP-Lec03).
    *   *Source*: SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec03.
2.  **Step 2: Technical Stage Analysis & Relative Strength (RS) Scanning**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Stan Weinstein 4-Stage Analysis, MarketSmith RS Rating, Custom Sub-Sector Index Scans, StockBee 4% Scan, Scan Match Intersection.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Require Price \\(>\\) upward-sloping 30-week EMA with Green Weekly Vstop (TVGP-Lec02); MarketSmith RS Rating \\(> 70–80\\) (TVGP-Lec02, SOIC-Lec04); StockBee 4% Scan: daily gain \\(\ge 4\%\\), volume \\(>100\text{k}\\) and \\(>50\\)-day average (Course-Lec07); restrict selection to ₹2,500–₹20,000 crore market cap companies (SOIC-Lec06).
    *   *Source*: TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec03, SOIC-Lec04, SOIC-Lec06, Course-Lec07.
3.  **Step 3: Growth Catalyst Verification & Operational Screening**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: "EXPANDING" Growth Checklist, CATALYST Framework, CapEx ROCE Hierarchy, Con-Call LLM Variant Perception Extraction, Congrats Index.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Require YoY top-line revenue growth \\(>15\%\\) across 2–3 quarters (TVGP-Lec05); YoY PAT growth \\(>20\%–30\%\\) (TVGP-Lec05); active PAT growth target \\(\ge 2\text{x}–3\text{x}\\) index growth (minimum 18%+) (Course-Lec07); capacity expansion multiplier \\(>1.5\text{x}\\) (Course-Lec07); prioritize debottlenecking/brownfield CapEx over greenfield (TVGP-Lec05).
    *   *Source*: SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05, SOIC-Lec06, Course-Lec07.
4.  **Step 4: Forensic Accounting & Credit Rating Audit**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Credit Rating Agency Forensic Audit, Small-Cap Bear Filter, Deleveraging Screen.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Audit cash flow statements from rating reports to verify zero netted acquisition outflows, zero undisclosed recourse bill discounting, and positive operating cash flow (\\(>₹100\text{ cr}\\) for small-caps) (SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec02); confirm debt today \\(<\\) debt 1 year ago (TVGP-Lec05).
    *   *Source*: SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec05.
5.  **Step 5: Promoter & Management Quality Evaluation**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: 10-Year Retained Capital Deployment Formula (\\(1 - \frac{1}{(1+r)^{10}}\\)), Traffic Light Governance Framework, 6 Softer Warning Indicators.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Require Green Light capital allocation (core reinvestment, promoter-excluded buybacks, \\(\ge 60\%\\) asset sale dividend payouts) (TVGP-Lec08); verify sandbagged guidance (guided growth \\(<\\) delivered growth) (TVGP-Lec08); reject Red Light practices (unlisted side-cars, \\(>80\%\\) whistleblower complaint surge, hyper-precise decimal EPS guidance) (TVGP-Lec08).
    *   *Source*: TVGP-Lec08, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec12.
6.  **Step 6: Sector-Specific Metric Matching & Forward P&L Modeling**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Sequential P&L Waterfall Mechanics, Industry Outperformance Growth Multiplier, Banking ROA Tree, Cyclical P/B Anchor, Auto Ancillary Double Engine, Formula-Linked Claude AI Modeling.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Banking: \\(\text{ROA} > 1.0\%–1.15\%\\), \\(\text{ROE} > 14\%–15\%\\), \\(\text{GNPA} < 3\%\\), \\(\text{NNPA} < 1\%\\) (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec12); Metals/Cyclicals: buy at \\(\text{P/B} < 1.0\text{x}–1.3\text{x}\\) near trough margins, sell at \\(\text{P/B} > 2.5\text{x}\\) or \\(\text{EV/EBITDA} > 6\text{x}\\) (TVGP-Lec12); Auto Ancillaries: confirm OEM market share gains + kit-value expansion at 29%–30% EBITDA margins (TVGP-Lec12); apply depreciation heuristic (5.3%–5.92% of sales) and standard 25% tax baseline (SOIC-Lec13).
    *   *Source*: SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec12, SOIC-Lec13.
7.  **Step 7: Valuation Safety Net & Payoff Matrix Execution**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: Growth-to-P/E Parity Framework, Double Engine Model, 3-Scenario Payoff Matrix (Bull, Base, Bear), 2-Year Forward PEG Ratio, Reverse DCF.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Entry P/E sweet spot = 15x–35x P/E where expected 2-year PAT growth \\(\% \ge \text{P/E}\\) (TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11); 2-Year Forward \\(\text{PEG} \le 1.5\text{x}–1.75\text{x}\\) (SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11, SOIC-Lec13); avoid static \\(\text{P/E} > 50\text{x}\\) unless EPS growth \\(>100\%\\) (SOIC-Lec09); run Reverse DCF at a 12.0% return hurdle rate to solve implied PAT growth (SOIC-Lec13).
    *   *Source*: SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11, SOIC-Lec13.
8.  **Step 8: Portfolio Construction, Position Sizing & Exit Discipline**
    *   *Tool/Metric*: 2-4-6-8 Position Sizing Model, 200 DMA Sentiment Gauge, 11-Stage Market Emotion Cycle, Peak Exit Framework, Time & Technical Stop-Loss Rules.
    *   *Threshold/Condition*: Portfolio sizing: 2% moonshots, 4%–6% medium conviction, 6%–8% high conviction (Course-Lec07); total holdings = 15–20 core stocks (maximum 25–30) (TVGP-Lec05, TVGP-Lec11, TVGP-Lec12); market timing: panic buy when \\(<20\%–25\%\\) stocks \\(>\\) 200 DMA, euphoria exit when \\(>85\%–90\%\\) (TVGP-Lec11); technical exit: weekly close below 30-week or 40-week EMA (TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec08); time stop-loss: exit if thesis fails for 6–8 quarters (6–12 months for CapEx) (Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec08); Peak exit: reduce/exit when Peak P/E, Peak Margins, and Peak Narrative coincide (TVGP-Lec10); "When in doubt, sell half" (Course-Lec07).
    *   *Source*: Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec08, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11, TVGP-Lec12.

### 8. COMMON MISTAKES & RED FLAGS ACROSS THE MODULE

*   **Commodity Peak Margin & Overcapacity Traps**: Purchasing commoditized manufacturing businesses (solar cells, ethanol, amine chemicals, packaging films) when operating EBITDA margins sit at historical lifetime highs or global capacity massively exceeds demand. Promoters overbuild, prices crash (e.g., solar modules \\(-50\%\\), acetonitrile \\(<\text{₹}100/\text{kg}\\)), and trailing low P/E multiples de-rate into severe capital traps (SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec02, SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05, SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec12).
*   **Chasing High Entry Multiples & Growth Traps (\\(>30\text{x}–50\text{x}\\) P/E)**: Paying \\(>50\text{x}\\) P/E (or \\(>30\text{x}–35\text{x}\\) with decelerating PAT) locks investors into multi-year time or price corrections when top-line growth decelerates due to base effects or competition. Moats without growth generate zero returns, stripping growth premiums down to baseline bond yield multiples (\\(\sim 14\text{x}–15\text{x}\\) P/E) (SOIC-Lec01, SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05, SOIC-Lec06, SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11).
*   **Ignoring Technical Stage 4 Downtrends & Parabolic Extensions**: Practicing "diamond hands" or averaging down on stocks trading below a downward-sloping 30-week EMA or triggering a red Weekly Vstop signal. Buying parabolic tops extended $70\%–80\%$ above the 150-day DMA during retail euphoria leads to severe drawdowns (TVGP-Lec02, SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05, Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec08, TVGP-Lec10).
*   **SME & Micro-Cap Illiquidity & Information Asymmetry**: Purchasing SME stocks or micro-caps ($<\text{₹}2,500\text{ crore}$ market cap) at peak multiples ($80\text{x}$ P/E or $10\text{x}$ sales) based solely on top-line projections. Floating stock gets cornered on the way up, but suffers $40\%–50\%$ drawdowns with zero buyers during market corrections, compounded by lack of promoter access and layered accounting manipulation (SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec03, SOIC-Lec06, Course-Lec07, TVGP-Lec10).
*   **Promoter Governance Red Flags & Capital Allocation Missteps**: Tolerating unlisted side-car entities that siphon primary sector growth away from listed shareholders, over-promotional media posturing, hyper-precise decimal EPS guidance, post-IPO dilution, whistleblower complaint surges ($>80\%$), or massive CapEx funded by high debt where CapEx $\ge$ Market Cap (SOIC-Lec01, TVGP-Lec05, TVGP-Lec08, TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec11, TVGP-Lec12).
*   **Mistaking Transitory Windfalls or Other Income for Structural Growth**: Capitalizing short-lived profit surges (refining spread spikes, COVID vaccine windfalls, seasonal RAC AC booms) or non-operational other income as recurring core earnings, leading to elevated base effects and severe earnings torpedoes in subsequent quarters (SOIC-Lec04, TVGP-Lec05, Course-Lec07, SOIC-Lec09, TVGP-Lec11).
*   **Excessive Systemic Margin Leverage (MTF) & Pledging**: Utilizing Margin Trading Facility (MTF) leverage or holding stocks with promoter pledging $>28\%–29\%$ during market euphoria, triggering automated forced liquidations during sentiment drawdowns (TVGP-Lec10, TVGP-Lec12).
*   **B2G (Business-to-Government) Early Entry & Working Capital Lockups**: Purchasing B2G companies prior to official statutory policy notifications or order wins, leading to prolonged working capital lockups, receivable write-downs, and regulatory delays (TVGP-Lec02, TVGP-Lec08).
