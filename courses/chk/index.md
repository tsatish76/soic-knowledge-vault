## 2. Master TVGP Pre-Flight Checklist

Before capital is allocated in Sanchay, every holding must pass these minimum hurdles:

| Pillar | Core Question | Red Flag Threshold | Sanchay Gatekeeper Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Theme (T)** | Is the industry growing > 1.5x GDP? | Structurally declining or commoditized sector | Disallow if industry ROCE is trending down |
| **Valuation (V)** | What growth is priced in? | P/E in 90th percentile without earnings acceleration | Flag amber if PEG > 2.5x |
| **Growth (G)** | Can company double sales in 4–5 years? | Capex without internal cash accruals | OCF / EBITDA must be > 70% |
| **Promoter (P)** | Are minority shareholders protected? | Promoter pledge > 10% or related-party leaks | Reject if auditor notes are qualified |
