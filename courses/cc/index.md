# CC — Sub-Course Master Summary (`TVGP Class2-9: Crash Course`)

### 1\. OVERARCHING THEME & OBJECTIVE

The **TVGP Class 2–9: Crash Course (`CC`)** builds an end-to-end institutional equity research and execution system that equips retail investors to find, verify, value, size, and exit Indian multi-bagger stocks without relying on sell-side target prices or consensus narratives (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec07`). Across seven lectures (`CC-Lec01` to `CC-Lec07`), the curriculum trains the investor to isolate non-consensus **"variant perception"** inflections—such as unserved category white spaces, balance-sheet deleveraging, unabsorbed gross block operating leverage, and demergers (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec02`)—verify their authenticity through primary alternative datasets (EPFO payrolls, credit ratings, HS chemical codes, PharmaCompass) and three-statement cash-flow reconciliation (`CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec04`), value them across bull/base/bear scenarios, sector-specific multiples (`P/E`, `PEG`, `Forward P/E`, `EV/EBITDA`, `SOTP`, `P/CF`, `P/S`), and `DCF`/`Reverse DCF` models (`CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`), and execute entries and exits using Stan Weinstein's 4-Stage price-volume rules, multi-timeframe Relative Strength (`RS`), moving averages, and the Volatility Stop (`V-Stop`) (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec07`).

Within the **TVGP (Theme, Valuation, Growth, Promoter)** architecture, this sub-course integrates all four pillars into a unified decision engine (`CC-Lec01`–`CC-Lec07`). **Theme (`T`)** anchors the empirical rule that **60% of an individual stock's price return is driven by its sector trend**, teaching investors to ride sector-wide Capex, anti-dumping, or export tailwinds while tracking capital cycles and MarketSmith/TradingView Relative Strength (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`). **Growth (`G`)** is dissected through the 9 fundamental earnings catalysts, the operating leverage equation (\$\\text{Depreciation Growth} \> \\text{Sales Growth}\$; \$\\text{Target Gross Block} \\times \\text{Asset Turn}\$), working-capital release, and volatile-to-consistent earnings transitions (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec06`). **Promoter (`P`)** is audited via signaling theory (15%-upfront promoter warrants, maiden concalls), capital allocation (EPS/ROE-accretive share buybacks vs. ROE-destroying idle cash hoarding or unrelated hotels), forensic cash-flow conversion (`CFO/PAT`), and insider selling footprints on weekly volume charts (`CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec07`). **Valuation (`V`)** serves as the ultimate margin-of-safety gatekeeper across `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, and `CC-Lec07`, proving why "Buy at Any Price" (`BAP` \>50x–230x P/E) baskets underperform the Nifty 50 and enforcing strict quantitative guardrails (`PEG ≤ 1.5x–2.0x`, `Base Case CAGR > 15%`, `EV/EBITDA < 20–22x`, `P/CF < 22–23x`, `P/S < 3x`).

### 3\. CORE PILLARS OF THE MODULE

#### Pillar 1: Variant Perception, Catalyst Taxonomy & Primary Scuttlebutt (`[TVGP: Theme & Growth]`)

- **Why it matters:** Consensus headline narratives are already priced into equity valuations (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec02`). Outsized multi-bagger returns require identifying structural operational inflections—before they appear in trailing earnings—by cross-checking corporate signaling against independent industrial data (`CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec03`).  
- **Progression across lectures:** `CC-Lec01` (introduced via White Spaces, *Matsya Nyaya*, Anti-Dumping vacuums, Demergers, S-Curves, and PEAD screens) → `CC-Lec02` (applied via Signaling Theory, 10 Variant Perception catalysts, the BRM concall framework, and EPFO/PharmaCompass/SunSirs/Google Earth scuttlebutt) → `CC-Lec03` (refined into 9 Fundamental Growth Catalysts paired with the Catalyst Inversion Rule for structural exits and concall tone shifts such as Neuland's first "sustainable" guidance in 5 years) → `CC-Lec06` & `CC-Lec07` (synthesized into "NOS" re-rating catalysts, volatile-to-consistent earnings transitions, and capital-cycle QIP vs. Capex tracking).  
- **Key rules/thresholds:**  
  - **Idea Frequency Hurdle:** Wait for 1 to 2 fat-pitch ideas every 1 to 2 years within the ₹1,000 Cr–₹15,000 Cr market-cap sweet spot with 0% promoter pledge (`CC-Lec01`).  
  - **Promoter Warrant Signal:** 15% upfront cash payment with an 85% conversion window within \$\\le 18\$ months signals high insider conviction (`CC-Lec02`).  
  - **EPFO Payroll Scaling:** \$+30%\$ to \$+40%\$ YoY headcount growth on 7-digit EPFO establishment codes confirms real on-ground expansion (`CC-Lec02`).  
  - **Brownfield NCLT Acquisition Discount:** Acquiring stressed assets at \$\\sim 1/3\\text{rd}\$ (\$28%\\text{–}35%\$) of greenfield replacement cost acts as a high-margin-of-safety growth catalyst (`CC-Lec03`).  
- **Read more:** `CC-Lec01` (Stages 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 11), `CC-Lec02` (Stages 2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13), `CC-Lec03` (Stages 5, 8), `CC-Lec06` (Stages 1, 2, 3), `CC-Lec07` (Stage 2).

#### Pillar 2: Operating Leverage & Three-Statement Financial Integration (`[TVGP: Growth & Valuation]`)

- **Why it matters:** Accrual P\&L profits can be manipulated via aggressive credit sales or distorted by heavy upfront depreciation during capacity expansions (`CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec04`). Connecting the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement (`CFO`, `CFI`, `CFF`) allows an investor to distinguish genuine cash-generating operating leverage from working-capital traps that face imminent equity dilution or bankruptcy (`CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec06`).  
- **Progression across lectures:** `CC-Lec01` (introduced via the \$\\text{CWIP} \> \\text{Net Block}\$ screen and the Single Master Screen Blunder warning) → `CC-Lec02` (applied via the Operating Leverage Heuristic where \$\\text{Depreciation Growth} \> \\text{Sales Growth}\$ and \$\\text{Latent Revenue} \= \\text{Target Gross Block} \\times \\text{Asset Turn}\$) → `CC-Lec04` (deepened across 12 stages connecting Gross Block, CWIP, non-cash depreciation add-backs, NWC days, DuPont ROCE, and 5-year CFO vs. PAT/Debt tests) → `CC-Lec06` & `CC-Lec07` (integrated into `Price-to-Cash-Flow [P/CF]` screening, Bull/Base/Bear depreciation/interest modeling, and cyclical trough-to-peak EBITDA valuation).  
- **Key rules/thresholds:**  
  - **Capacity Utilization Capex Trigger:** Discretionary Capex is triggered when utilization crosses **70%–75%** (`CC-Lec02`); greenfield hospitals require **5 to 6 years** to break even (`CC-Lec04`).  
  - **Future Revenue Power Formula:** \$(\\text{Current Gross Block} \+ \\text{Planned Capex}) \\times \\text{Asset Turn}\$ (e.g., 1.5x in specialty chemicals like Aarti Industries; \~10x in asset-light transformers like Shilchar Technologies) (`CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec04`).  
  - **Minimum CFO-to-PAT Conversion Floors:** **B2B businesses \$\\ge 60%\\text{–}70%\$**; **B2C businesses \$\\ge 80%\\text{–}90%\$** (`CC-Lec04`).  
  - **Net Working Capital (NWC) Days & DuPont ROCE:** \$\\text{NWC Days} \= \\text{Receivable Days} \+ \\text{Inventory Days} \- \\text{Payable Days}\$; shrinking NWC days reduces Capital Employed (\$\\text{Fixed Assets} \+ \\text{Working Capital}\$) and automatically boosts \$\\text{ROCE} \= (\\text{EBIT}/\\text{Sales}) \\times (\\text{Sales}/\\text{Capital Employed})\$ (`CC-Lec04`).  
- **Read more:** `CC-Lec01` (Stages 7, 11, 12), `CC-Lec02` (Stages 4, 5, 6, 7, 11), `CC-Lec04` (Stages 1–8, 11, 12), `CC-Lec06` (Stage 6), `CC-Lec07` (Stages 3, 7).

#### Pillar 3: Capital Allocation, Promoter Integrity & Forensic Guardrails (`[TVGP: Promoter]`)

- **Why it matters:** Institutional Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) apply strict governance vetoes against promoters who misallocate capital into unrelated diversification, hoard unproductive cash, or fail forensic cash-flow checks (`CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec07`). Conversely, clean governance, debt reduction, and value-accretive share buybacks drive sustained multiple re-rating (`CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec05`).  
- **Progression across lectures:** `CC-Lec01` (introduced via 0% promoter pledge filters, Usha Martin's steel division divestiture, and Eicher Motors divesting 13 non-core businesses) → `CC-Lec02` & `CC-Lec03` (applied via promoter warrant analysis, credit-rating de-risking checks, Shivalik Bimetal's 10% promoter dumping volume bar, and rejecting 6-year cash-flow laggards or Rajesh Exports) → `CC-Lec04` (modeled across all three statements for share buybacks in GPIL/Infosys/TCS vs. Avanti Feeds' 50% idle cash drag and Bartronics/Billcare/Opto Circuits receivable bankruptcies) → `CC-Lec05` & `CC-Lec07` (refined into Intrinsic vs. Transactional Value, cornered-float traps, and DII governance vetoes on Balaji Amines' hotel project and Garware Hi-Tech's ₹50 Cr subsidiary charge).  
- **Key rules/thresholds:**  
  - **Share Buyback Three-Statement Effect:** Outflow in `CFF` \+ share extinguishment raising `EPS` on P\&L \+ reduction in Cash and Shareholders' Equity (\$\\text{Share Capital} \+ \\text{Reserves}\$) mathematically boosting \$\\text{ROE} \= \\text{PAT} \\div \\text{Equity}\$ (`CC-Lec04`).  
  - **Idle Cash Drag Warning:** Parking \$\\sim 50%\$ of total assets in \$\\sim 5%\$-yielding mutual funds/cash with a 20%–30% dividend payout and no Capex plan compresses consolidated ROE (e.g., 24% down to 13% in Avanti Feeds) (`CC-Lec04`).  
  - **Forensic Rejection Rule:** Reject companies with **6 consecutive years** of poor/negative cash flows, frequent name changes, missing concalls, or \>8-year Stage 4 volume stagnation even if trading at 10x P/E (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec05`).  
- **Read more:** `CC-Lec01` (Stages 6, 7, 8, 11), `CC-Lec02` (Stages 2, 10, 11), `CC-Lec03` (Stages 4, 5, 11, 12), `CC-Lec04` (Stages 2, 3, 9, 10, 12), `CC-Lec05` (Stages 6, 7), `CC-Lec07` (Stage 2).

#### Pillar 4: Multi-Method Valuation Discipline & Scenario Modeling (`[TVGP: Valuation]`)

- **Why it matters:** Starting valuation dictates long-term compounding odds: empirical testing of a 21-stock "Buy at Any Price" (`BAP`, \>50x–60x P/E) basket showed it underperformed the Nifty 50 over 5 years (`94%` vs. `97.8%`, or `-8%` alpha above 50x–75x P/E) because minor earnings deceleration triggers severe P/E de-rating (`CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec07`). Matching the right valuation metric to the business model and requiring a \>15% Base Case CAGR protects the investor from the "overhang of overpaying" (`CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
- **Progression across lectures:** `CC-Lec01` (introduced via `<30x P/E` screening cutoffs and `PEG ~1.0x`) → `CC-Lec05` (established P/E re-rating vs. de-rating math, Earnings Yield, 5 trailing-P/E failure adjustments, stripping one-offs, and \$\\le 2\\text{-year}\$ Forward P/E) → `CC-Lec06` (expanded into the 10-year `34%–35%` earnings growth ceiling, `EV/EBITDA`, `SOTP`, `P/CF`, `P/S`, and the Five Valuation Guardrails) → `CC-Lec07` (culminated in 3–4 year Bull/Base/Bear P\&L modeling with Excel `RATE`, 10-year `DCF` Owner's Earnings \+ Gordon Terminal Value, `Reverse DCF` implied growth audits, and cyclical trough-to-peak EV/EBITDA modeling).  
- **Key rules/thresholds:**  
  - **The Five Valuation Guardrails (`CC-Lec06`, refined across `CC-Lec05` & `CC-Lec07`):**  
    1. **PEG Ratio:** \$\\le \\mathbf{1.5\\text{x}}\$ for most businesses (\$\\le \\mathbf{2.0\\text{x}}\$ ceiling reserved for consistent consumer businesses; sweet spot **0.6x–1.5x**) (`CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
    2. **Trailing / Entry P/E:** \$\<\\mathbf{30\\text{x}}\$ in screens (`CC-Lec01`), \$\<\\mathbf{35\\text{x}}\$ guardrail (`CC-Lec06`), **30x–40x** sweet spot (`CC-Lec07`), and **\>40x–45x** (especially **\>50x–75x** to **220–230x**) as the high-risk de-rating danger zone (`CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec07`).  
    3. **Price-to-Sales (`P/S`):** \$\<\\mathbf{3\\text{x}}\$ (or below historical median P/S at cyclical troughs; **2x P/S** at cyclical peak) (`CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
    4. **Price-to-Cash-Flow (`P/CF`):** \$\<\\mathbf{22\\text{x}–23\\text{x}}\$ (ideal \~10x P/CF when high depreciation masks cash profitability) (`CC-Lec06`).  
    5. **EV/EBITDA:** \$\<\\mathbf{20\\text{x}–22\\text{x}}\$ for asset-heavy sectors (hospitals, steel, cement; **7x–10x Peak EBITDA** for deep cyclicals); never use on asset-light platforms like CDSL/IEX (`CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
  - **Bull/Base/Bear Margin of Safety Hurdle:** Require **Base Case CAGR \> 15%** over 3–4 years (assuming zero P/E re-rating, \$\\text{Depreciation} \= \\text{Capex} \\div 10\\text{–}15\\text{ yrs}\$, and **9%** interest rate on incremental debt) (`CC-Lec07`).  
  - **10-Year DCF Parameters:** \$\\text{Owner's Earnings (FCF)} \= \\text{CFO} \- \\text{Maintenance Capex (Depreciation)}\$; Terminal Growth \$g \= \\mathbf{4%}\$; Discount Rate \$r \= \\mathbf{10%}\$ (vs. **6%** for foreign institutional investors) (`CC-Lec07`).  
- **Read more:** `CC-Lec01` (Stages 11, 14), `CC-Lec05` (Stages 1–10), `CC-Lec06` (Stages 1–7), `CC-Lec07` (Stages 1–5, 7).

#### Pillar 5: Multi-Timeframe Technical Execution, Relative Strength & Unemotional Exits (`[TVGP: Theme & Valuation]`)

- **Why it matters:** Fundamentals tell you *what* to buy, but technicals tell you *when* to buy, *how* to filter the 60% sector tailwind, and *when* to exit (`CC-Lec03`). Because **88% of winners from the previous bull cycle do not lead the next cycle** and take an average of **4 to 5 years** to recover, operating without objective exit rules turns long-term investors into **"Forced Long-Term Investors" (FLT)** enduring 60%–90% Stage 4 drawdowns (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec07`).  
- **Progression across lectures:** `CC-Lec01` & `CC-Lec02` (introduced Stan Weinstein's 4 Stages, rising 30-week EMA, and Relative Strength \> 0 on Laurus Labs) → `CC-Lec03` (built the complete technical system: 4 Price-Volume regimes, Selling Climax/"Ditch", Levy's 26-week RS study, TradingView Modulus RS lookbacks, MarketSmith `Growth 50` / `Blue Dot` screens, Darvas Box breakouts, the 4-part `SOIC LTI` buying setup, Donchian/PSAR pyramiding, and 3-tier EMA \+ `V-Stop` exits) → `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06` & `CC-Lec07` (integrated Weekly vs. Monthly `V-Stop` timeframes and Wyckoff late-stage base counting \[Bases \$\\ge 3\\text{–}5\$\] with valuation exit triggers).  
- **Key rules/thresholds:**  
  - **4-Part Stage 2 Weekly Buying Setup (`SOIC LTI`):** (1) Weekly Price \> upward-sloping **30-week EMA** (breaking out of a Stage 1 Darvas Box), (2) **Weekly RS (Modulus vs. `NSE:CNX500`, 13 or 26W) \> 0**, (3) **Weekly RSI \> 50**, and (4) **Weekly ADX \> 20** (`CC-Lec03`).  
  - **Multi-Layer Mechanical Exit System (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`):**  
    1. **Blow-Off Climax Extension Exit:** Sell 100% immediately when daily price stretches **\>80% to 100%+ above the 200-day EMA** (or **150-day EMA** in `CC-Lec07`, or surges **\+120%–130% in 2 months**) at 80x–120x P/E with climax volume (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec07`).  
    2. **Parabolic "Eiffel Tower" Exit (`10-Week EMA`):** For stocks up 2–3x in 3M or 5–6x in 1Y, sell **50% on the 1st weekly breakdown** below the 10-week EMA (or 20-week EMA in `CC-Lec07`) and **100% on the 2nd breakdown** (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec07`).  
    3. **Core 50/50 Dual Exit (`V-Stop` \+ `30-Week EMA`):** Sell **50%** when Weekly/Monthly **Volatility Stop (`Length = 10, Multiplier = 2.0` \[or `2.5` for high conviction in `CC-Lec03`; `1.5x` on Monthly / `1.5x–2.0x` in `CC-Lec06`–`CC-Lec07`\])** flips red, and sell the **remaining 50%** on a comprehensive **30-week EMA** breakdown (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
- **Read more:** `CC-Lec01` (Stage 10), `CC-Lec02` (Stage 4), `CC-Lec03` (Stages 1–12), `CC-Lec05` (Stage 9), `CC-Lec06` (Stages 2, 7), `CC-Lec07` (Stages 6, 7).

#### b) Curriculum Architecture & Pedagogical Progression

The sub-course is engineered so each lecture resolves the blind spots of the preceding stage. `CC-Lec01` and `CC-Lec02` establish *what* creates a multi-bagger—teaching learners to hunt for structural white spaces, capacity inflections (\$\\text{CWIP} \> \\text{Net Block}\$, \$\\text{Depreciation Growth} \> \\text{Sales Growth}\$), and concall variant perception validated through EPFO and credit-rating scuttlebutt. However, a compelling fundamental story bought in a primary downtrend or backed by fictitious credit sales will still destroy capital. Therefore, `CC-Lec03` introduces the complete technical timing and exit architecture (price-volume regimes, Relative Strength vs. Nifty 500, Stan Weinstein's 4 Stages, the 4-part `SOIC LTI` buying setup, and `10-W EMA` / `200-DMA` / `V-Stop` \+ `30-W EMA` exits), while `CC-Lec04` joins the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement so learners can audit whether reported PAT actually converts into `CFO` (\$\\ge 60%\\text{–}70%\$ B2B, \$\\ge 80%\\text{–}90%\$ B2C), whether Capex can be funded via internal accruals (`Pricol`), or whether bloated working capital will force equity dilution (`Neogen Chemicals`).

Once the learner can identify a forensically clean business entering a Stage 2 technical breakout, `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, and `CC-Lec07` construct a three-part valuation fortress to prevent overpaying. `CC-Lec05` dismantles the "Buy at Any Price" (`BAP`) fallacy using P/E de-rating math (`Infosys`, `DMart`, `Pidilite`, `Alkyl Amines`), strips out one-off earnings (`Aarti Industries`, `Natco Pharma`), and introduces \$\\le 2\\text{-year}\$ Forward P/E and `PEG`. `CC-Lec06` expands the toolkit beyond P/E by matching valuation metrics to asset intensity—introducing `EV/EBITDA` and `SOTP` for asset-heavy businesses and conglomerates (`Laurus Labs`, `SRF`, `Hospitals`), `P/CF` for high-depreciation operating leverage (`Sandhar`), and `P/S` for deep cyclicals (`Thirumalai`), codified into five hard valuation guardrails. Finally, `CC-Lec07` integrates all prior modules into forward-looking **Bull/Base/Bear P\&L scenario models** (requiring \$\>15%\$ Base Case CAGR), **10-year DCF / Reverse DCF** expectations tests (`Varun Beverages`, `Kaynes Technology`), **cyclical trough-to-peak EV/EBITDA models**, **Wyckoff late-stage base counting** (Bases \$\\ge 3\\text{–}5\$), and **Core/Satellite portfolio sizing** (`2-4-6-8%` rule).

### 5\. MASTER FRAMEWORK / CHECKLIST

1. **Step 1 — Thematic & Modular Quantitative Screening (`[TVGP: Theme & Growth]`):**  
   - Run modular (never combined all-in-one) Screener.in queries targeting specific catalysts inside the **₹1,000 Cr–₹15,000 Cr market-cap sweet spot** with **0% promoter pledge**: (a) **Deleveraging Screen** (\$\\text{Current Debt} \< \\text{Debt}\_{3\\text{Yrs Ago}}\$, 3Yr PAT CAGR \$\>15%\$, rising ROCE, \$\\text{P/E} \< 30\\times\$), (b) **PEAD Drift Screen** (Quarterly YoY Sales \$\>20%\$ AND PAT \$\>20%\$, \$\\text{P/E} \< 30\\times\$), or (c) **Capex Inflection Screen** (\$\\text{CWIP} \> \\text{Net Block}\$ or \$\\text{Depreciation Growth} \> \\text{Sales Growth}\$) (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec02`).  
   - Simultaneously run technical relative-strength screens on **MarketSmith India `Growth 50`** (\$\\text{RS Rating} \\ge 80\$) and **`Blue Dot` list** (\$\\text{RS Rating} \\ge 75\$) or TradingView (`RS > 0`) to align with the **60% sector tailwind rule** (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec04`).  
2. **Step 2 — Variant Perception, Signaling & Primary Scuttlebutt Audit (`[TVGP: Growth & Promoter]`):**  
   - Read quarterly conference calls and credit rating reports using the **BRM (Business, Risk, Management)** model to identify at least one of the **9–10 structural catalysts** (new product, geographic/distribution expansion, client mining, operating leverage at \$\>70%\\text{–}75%\$ utilization, brownfield acquisition at \$\\sim 1/3\\text{rd}\$ replacement cost, maiden concall, or promoter warrants with **15% upfront cash and \$\\le 18\\text{-month}\$ conversion**) (`CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec03`).  
   - Cross-verify physical execution via **EPFO payroll filings** (targeting \$+30%\\text{ to }+40%\$ YoY headcount growth), **PharmaCompass / SunSirs / HS codes** (verifying specialty API realizations \$\>\$2,000\\text{–}\$5,000/\\text{kg}\$ vs. cyclical over-earning spikes), and **Google Earth** satellite imagery (`CC-Lec02`).  
3. **Step 3 — Three-Statement Forensic & Capital Allocation Verification (`[TVGP: Promoter & Growth]`):**  
   - Reconcile 3-to-5-year cumulative **Cash Flow from Operations (`CFO`)** against cumulative **PAT** and **Total Debt**: enforce **\$\\text{CFO/PAT} \\ge 60%\\text{–}70%\$ for B2B** and **\$\\ge 80%\\text{–}90%\$ for B2C** (and \$\\text{CFO/EBITDA} \\approx 80%\$ in the SOIC Forensic Sheet) while rejecting firms with 6 years of negative cash flows or missing concalls (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec05`).  
   - Audit **Net Working Capital Days** (\$\\text{Receivable} \+ \\text{Inventory} \- \\text{Payable Days}\$) and **3-year CFO vs. guided Capex (`CFI`)** to confirm whether growth is self-funded via internal accruals (`Pricol`) or faces QIP dilution (`Neogen Chemicals`), and check for governance red flags (unrelated hotels, subsidiary write-offs, cornered \~90% floats, or \$\\sim 50%\$ assets sitting in idle cash/mutual funds) (`CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec07`).  
4. **Step 4 — Multi-Method Valuation & Bull/Base/Bear Scenario Modeling (`[TVGP: Valuation]`):**  
   - Strip out non-recurring one-offs (contract termination fees, COVID/FTF windfalls) to compute **Core Adjusted P/E** and **2-Year Forward P/E**, and match the multiple to the business model against the **Five Valuation Guardrails**: **\$\\text{PEG} \\le 1.5\\text{x}\$** (\$\\le 2.0\\text{x}\$ consumer), **\$\\text{P/E} \< 35\\text{x}\$** (30x–40x sweet spot; avoid \$\>50\\text{x}\$ BAP zone), **\$\\text{P/S} \< 3\\text{x}\$**, **\$\\text{P/CF} \< 22\\text{x}–23\\text{x}\$**, and **\$\\text{EV/EBITDA} \< 20\\text{x}–22\\text{x}\$** (or **SOTP** for conglomerates; **7x–10x Peak EBITDA** on \$\\text{Gross Block} \\times \\text{Turn} \\times \\text{Peak OPM}\$ for cyclicals) (`CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
   - Build a 3–4 year **Bull/Base/Bear P\&L model** (deducting \$\\text{Capex} \\div 10\\text{–}15\\text{ yrs}\$ for depreciation and **9%** interest on incremental debt) and test via Excel `RATE(nper, 0, -Current_MCap, Future_MCap)` that the **Base Case CAGR exceeds 15%** without relying on P/E re-rating; cross-check high-multiple compounders via **10-Year DCF** (\$r \= 10%, g \= 4%\$) or **Reverse DCF** (`CC-Lec07`).  
5. **Step 5 — Stage 2 Technical Entry, Position Sizing & Pyramiding (`[TVGP: Theme & Valuation]`):**  
   - Never buy in **Stage 4** (price below falling 30-week EMA) and avoid late-stage post-QIP bases (**Base \$\\ge 3\\text{–}5\$**) (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec07`). Enter on a high-volume breakout from a **Stage 1 Darvas rectangular base** when all **4 weekly `SOIC LTI` conditions** align: (1) \$\\text{Price} \> \\text{upward-sloping 30-week EMA}\$, (2) \$\\text{Weekly RS (13/26W vs. Nifty 500)} \> 0\$ (or `3W RS` \+ `9–10W EMA` for recent IPOs), (3) \$\\text{Weekly RSI} \> 50\$, and (4) \$\\text{Weekly ADX} \> 20\$ (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec07`).  
   - Size positions across a **15–25 stock portfolio** (hard ceiling: 30\) using the **Dinner Analogy / `2-4-6-8%` Rule**: **6%–8% (max 10%)** in high-conviction Core compounders, **2%–4%** in tactical Satellite/cyclical plays, and **\$\\le 10%\\text{–}12%\$ total portfolio weight** in SME/frothy stocks (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec07`). Pyramid into winning Stage 2 positions on **20-period Donchian Channel** upper-band candle cuts or **Parabolic SAR (`0.02, 0.2`)** positive flips (`CC-Lec03`).  
6. **Step 6 — Unemotional Multi-Layer Exit Execution (`[TVGP: Valuation & Growth]`):**  
   - **Parabolic / Climax Top Exit:** Sell 100% immediately if daily price stretches **\$\>80%\\text{ to }100%+\$ above the 200-day (or 150-day) EMA** on climax volume at 80x–120x P/E, or surges **\$+120%\\text{–}130%\$ in 2 months**; on weekly parabolic "Eiffel Tower" charts, sell **50% on the 1st `10-week EMA` (or `20-week EMA`) break** and **100% on the 2nd break** (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec07`).  
   - **Core Long-Term 50/50 Dual Exit & Catalyst Inversion:** Sell **50%** when Weekly/Monthly **`V-Stop` (`Length = 10, Multiplier = 2.0` \[or `1.5x–2.5x`\])** turns red and the **remaining 50%** when price breaks comprehensively below the **`30-week EMA`**, or exit immediately when the original **fundamental catalyst inverts** (competitor Capex glut, client slowdown, or governance shock) (`CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).

### 8\. COMMON MISTAKES & RED FLAGS ACROSS THE MODULE

- **The "Buy at Any Price" (`BAP`) & Linear Extrapolation Trap:** Paying \$\>50\\text{x}–230\\text{x}\$ P/E for quality stocks (`DMart`, `Pidilite`, `Relaxo`, `Fine Organics`, `Kaynes Technology`, `Infosys` in 2000\) or extrapolating temporary 2–3 year windfalls (COVID diagnostics/drugs in `Divi's`/`Dr. Lal`, propiconazole pricing in `Astec LifeSciences`, anti-dumping duty in `Borosil Renewables`, or Revlimid in `Natco Pharma`) leads to multi-year time and price corrections when growth mean-reverts (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec02`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
- **Becoming a "Forced Long-Term Investor" (FLT) & Buying in Stage 4:** Refusing to execute `V-Stop` and `30-week EMA` exits when a multi-bagger breaks down traps investors in 60%–90% Stage 4 collapses (`Laurus Labs`, `DLF`, `Delta Corp`, `Sequent Scientific`), while premature bottom-fishing right after a selling climax before the 30-week EMA flattens into Stage 1 inflicts immediate 20% cuts (`Aarti Industries` at ₹480) (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
- **Ignoring Accrual-to-Cash Divergence & Single-Customer Working Capital Traps:** Trusting 80%–100% P\&L revenue growth without checking `CFO/PAT` conversion (\$\\ge 60%\\text{–}70%\$ B2B, \$\\ge 80%\\text{–}90%\$ B2C) exposes investors to receivable frauds (`Opto Circuits`, `Bartronics`, `Billcare`) or relentless QIP dilution (`Neogen Chemicals`, `MTAR Technologies`) (`CC-Lec04`, `CC-Lec06`).  
- **The Single Master Screen Blunder & Mismatched Valuation Multiples:** Combining heavy Capex with high current ROCE and expanding margins in a single screen filters out the best operating-leverage turnarounds during their commissioning lag (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec04`). Similarly, using `EV/EBITDA` on asset-light platforms (`IEX`, `CDSL`) or trailing `P/E` on deep cyclicals at trough (\~1,800x P/E) or peak (low optical P/E) produces inverted, value-destroying decisions (`CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec06`, `CC-Lec07`).  
- **Retail Past-Price Anchoring vs. Late-Stage Post-QIP Euphoria:** Anchoring to a stock's lower historical price causes retail investors to miss early Stage 2 inflections (`Neuland Labs`, `Eicher Motors`), whereas chasing late-stage **Base 4–6 breakouts** immediately after QIPs at peak per-ton margins (`Apar Industries`) or buying \~90% cornered floats (`Divgi TorqTransfer`, `Metro Brands`) traps buyers at the cycle top (`CC-Lec01`, `CC-Lec03`, `CC-Lec05`, `CC-Lec07`).
