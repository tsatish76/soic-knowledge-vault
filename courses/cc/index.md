## Master Consolidated Rules

| Rule / Metric | Benchmark Threshold | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)** | < 45 Days for FMCG / Retail; < 75 Days for Capital Goods | Shorter CCC allows self-funded growth without debt dilution |
| **OCF to EBITDA Conversion** | > 75% Cumulative over 3 years | Low conversion reveals aggressive revenue recognition or stuck inventory |
| **Fixed Asset Turnover (FAT)** | > 2.5x upon full capacity utilization | Determines capital efficiency of new manufacturing facilities |
